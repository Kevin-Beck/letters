                                                  V-E Day
Dear Mum,
     Received Geo. letter yesterday along with five others. It is good to hear
from the rest of the family now and then.

     Have been listening to the radio about all day. It is an anti-climax but
still wonderful news. I am celebrating by staying in to-night.

     My day is routine now - Exercise in the morning, a walk in the afternoon,
and movie in the evening. Have been doing some reading between times. It is
rather monotonous but I imagine I can survive the ordeal. I still haven't any
idea when I will be discharged.

[--- page 2 ---]

     The Doc gave me permission to go to Portland to-morrow to get the car. I
would [rather, inserted] have it down here in case of some sudden move. Also I
want to see some of the people up there. There was a ship that used to tie up
at the pier whose officers I knew well and liked. A week ago it was sunk with
very few survivors. I want to know more about it.

     Fibber and Molly are on the air and I have run out of air so will close.

     Will still let you in on the developments as soon as I know them.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
