                                                  Sunday May 9
Dear Mother,
     As per usual this was just another day out here and one that I wish I
could have spent at home. I must admit that to-night I am thinking of you and
home - much more than than ordinarily - and accompaning that thought is a bit
of homesickness. There is one nice thing about our work that I like and
[crossed out] it is that we don't have much time to think of other things. It
is a very weak consolation.

     We did get one afternoon for recreation so we hit the beach and all went
ashore. We had some beer on ice and took that with us. I played some foot ball
and soft ball and found out that I have a few muscles that weren't being used.
I am still suffering every time I go up or down a ladder. I will gladly

[--- 430509pg2.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

suffer the after effects for a few hours relaxation now and again.

     The Captain was sick for a few days and the Eng. Officer & myself were
running the ship. It was such a difficult job but it made too much work for
me. He is o.k. now and I am begining to get back in shape. I will be quite
thankful when we hit Port again.

     I didn't realize it was so late when I started this letter. It is after
10 now and the rest are in bed trying to sleep so I had better turn in. I am
sorry this letter is so short but I will try to write a longer one in a few
days.
                                        Love to all,
                                             Tom
