                                                  17 Dec. 1943
Dear Mother,
     I haven't heard from you for several days but I am not complaining. I
have had quite a bit of work to do the last week and am still busy. I am away
behind in my letter writing and now have a lot of gifts to acknowledge. In
case you are interested here is the list,
     Florella            - toilet articles, towel, wash cloth
     Women's Mission Soc. - toilet articles
     Bertha & Carl       - Candy
     Luke & Lena         - Books, candy,
     Geo.                - Books
     Janny               - Watch straps
     H. Ford [?]         - Books
     Pelkey's            - Fruit Cake
I think that includes every thing. Your fruit cake arrived also.

     I don't know just how to explain this. When Dad's picture arrived I had
such a strange sensation. I often think of him and now his picture brings so
many more

worried but did want to know.

     Our old skipper should be in the States by now. I hope we don't change
commands often. There is a lot of paper work and changes involved. It looks
like I might be heading State-side when my 18 months are up. There are too
many ifs to the deal to even think about it.

     This may reach you by Christmas if it makes all connections. I will take a
chance that it will and I hope you enjoy the day as much as possible. With the
limits on traveling it should be like an old fashion Christmas with every one
staying home. I hope the weather man doesn't crosse me up because I will be
thinking of the day as one with lots of new fallen snow. White Christmases are
the best.
                                        All My Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck
