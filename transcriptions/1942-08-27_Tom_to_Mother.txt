                                                  August 27, 1942
Dear Mother,
     The reason for the pencil & paper is that class taking a final. It is
double one - seamanship & communications - but it is very short and fairly
easy. We have to stay in class the full two-hours so the instructor suggested
writing a letter home. I had the navigation final this morning and it didn't
seemed to be so hard. I finish it in two-hours. Every week we have a two-hour
quiz and I never finished one, so this one to-day was either easy or I knew
what I was doing. What makes this test I am taking now appear easy is we
don't have flag hoists nor any blinker.

     Tomorrow I have two more test - Damage Control & Ordinance. I don't know
what they will be like. So far neither one has bothered me.

     I received a letter from Betty to-day and she and John are thinking of
coming to New York in September. I will have to wait a few days to see how
the watch bills turn out. Will you explain to them the watch system, etc. I
hope we can make arrangements of some kind. For a few days after you were
here I felt fine and got a lot of work done. After that I went back into a
slump so I guess I need someone to pep me up once in awhile.

[--- 420827pg2.jpg ---]

     A new gang came into Johnson Hall to-day. I think it is a bunch of
midshipmen who were indoctrinated at Notre Dame. I just met some fellows at
Furnald who I knew at State. One is a Beta, but a good fellow. The fellows at
Furnald are getting something we didn't get and I am not sorry. They [crossed
out] P.T. [?] exercise for two hours every day. One cold morning we had to do
two laps around the field and I played out on one. I would be dead if I had
to do it for two hours.

     We will be released in a few minutes so I will close. I will try to
write again Saturday.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
