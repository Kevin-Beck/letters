                                                  Friday morning
                                                  [later hand: 1943]
Dear Mother,
     I thought it was about time I let you know how things are going. We
haven't done anything but sit since we came. We're just playboys 24 hrs. a day.
I suppose it's all right as we are getting aquainted with each other pretty
well. I don't like the outfit of boys this fall as well as June. But there are
some real nice boys.

     I didn't get the room I thought I was. There was a mixup and I got a big
single on the first floor on the lower side. I think it's a nicer room than the
other but Caflisch is full and they are expecting some other boys in soon, so I
expect to get a room mate which will make it plenty crowded. Some of the single
rooms in this section have already been doubled up and mine is one of the
largest down there. But if they do double up I will save $25.

     There was a dance last night in Brooks for the Freshmen. They didn't allow
dates and had different methods for getting blind partners. It was alot of fun
but I sure drew some lemons. I haven't met Pat Painter and have about given up.

[--- ZGeoAlleghenypg2.jpg ---]

We had name cards last night so I got 2 of my friends to keep watching for her.
When the dance was about three fourths over one of the boys saw her and told me
what she had on. I saw about five dressed to fit that discription. By the
process of elimination I got a look at her but I never meet (met) her. I feel
like I'm making a fool of myself asking every Tom, Dick & Harry if he knows Pat
Painter. If she is told by some of the boys that someone is hunting for her,
she'll think I'm a bit of a drip.

     I don't think I'll be home until next weekend and maybe not then as I
don't have my scedule yet.

     Could you or someone down home get me Bill's & Jim's full addresses. I'm
not certain about them. My room number is 43 this year.

     That's about all for today and I'll write again soon
                                        Love George
