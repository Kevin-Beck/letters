                                                  11-1-42
Dear Mother,
     The trip down here was nice and I am glad I came with Chet. He knows a
fellow in the OPA and this guy took us to lunch and let us use his apartment
to get cleaned up. We took a night boat down here and arrived around 0800.
Chet also knows a Lieutenant here so this fellow comes down in a station wagon
and picks us up, waits while we check in, takes us to lunch, and then on a
tour of the place. He wanted to take us to Virginia Beach last night but we
had a muster at 8 P.M.

     I don't think they were quite ready for us. The first eighty went to
Little Creek, the next to Solomons, and the rest of us remain here for a
while. Some of the fellows are getting transfers. The latest I have heard is
that we

Right now I am at the Bachelor Officer Quarters at Naval Operating Base. The
quarters [crossed out] are new, not completely furnished, and very nice. The
Base is a mamoth thing about 10 miles from town. The transportation is
terrible so we spend the evening at the Officer's Club. I never saw so much
gold braid in one place in all my life. The meals they serve are very good
and very reasonable.

     I may be station some other place by the time you get this letter but I
think the address will be the same and they will forward the mail. If you
want to take a chance the address is -

          Amphibious Force, Post Office
          Hampton Roads Branch,
          Naval Operating Base
          Norfolk, Virginia

It probably will take a couple of weeks for me to get a letter. Don't send
any clothes until I am sure of my address.
     That is all for now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
