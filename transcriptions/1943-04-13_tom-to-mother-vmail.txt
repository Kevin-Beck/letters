[V-MAIL form, No. 1116. Censor's stamp: "PASSED BY NAVAL CENSOR" (initials)]

To:    MRS. JOHN A. BECK
       KARNS CITY,
       PENNSYLVANIA
       USA

From:  Ens. Thos. F. Beck
       USS LCI(L) 62
       c/o P. M. San Francisco, Cal.
       April 13, 1943

Dear Mother,
     I sent a cablegram a few days ago and I suppose that you received it. If
not this won't make much sense. Anyway, after I sent I got to thinking about
and wasn't too sure if it was a good idea. I hope you weren't the least bit
scared when it came. After all some good news does travel by telegram. Don't
be surprised if you receive a few more now and then, I am confident that they
will still be good news.

     While underway I haven't much time for letter writing but when we hit
port I usually plenty time and plenty of desire, but very little material to
write about. The last time I got the urge I wrote to Uncle Chauncey, Betty,
Luke, and a couple of others. The reason I didn't write you was that I thought
the cablegram would be sufficient and it just about covered every thing I had
to say. I have afew more to get off while I am here: 711 E. Pearl, Adam, The
Becks, and of course a line or two to Mari and Sally. If we receive our mail
here I may have a few more to answer and I will also have an idea what to
write about. There may be something that you want to know and there may be a
slim chance that I will be able to tell you. I am getting quite anxious to
know what has been going on around home for the last few months. (My typing
is getting worse instead of better.)

     I have been thinking about making out an allottment for about half my pay
but can't make up my mind. So far I haven't had much use for money and I
don't like to carry too much with me, but then, I might get a fews days off
(very doubtful) and I would hate to get caught short. If I become too heavily
laden with greenbacks I can always send home either a bank draft or a money
order. I think I will wait until I get an excess and then send it home. By
the way, did you ever receive the money I sent from Galveston. I think it was
$200. I still have the receipts in case you didn't receive yours. Right now
money is one of my least worries.

     I am typing this in the radio room and am trying to listen to some of the
war news and now it sounds good on all fronts. I hope it has been just as
good or better for the last few weeks. I haven't listened to the radio for
quite a long time. I also heard some sports news and was a little surprised
to realized that it is baseball season. There has also been some music which
really sounded swell. Right now they are playing a waltz which I always did
like.

     I think I will go to bed early tonight and attempt to get a full nights
sleep. It will seem unusual and I probably wake up during the night and get
ready to go on watch. Going without sleep is getting to be a habit with me. I
am glad I had some experience along that line before I joined the Navy. Also
I expect to have a rather big day tomorrow, getting the ship really
shipshape. It is really hard work just overseeing all the work.

     Will write again soon and will not wait until I have any news.

                                        Love to all,
                                             Tom
