                                                  April 1, 1943
Dear Mother,

     We are in a port where there is a postoffice and I understand that
AirMail will reach the States in about 10 days so I thought I would write
again although as usual there is little to say. We never know how long it
takes mail to get home so I will write as often as possible and porbably the
last letters will reach you first. I am still wondering how the V-mail got
through.

     I am still a little tired from our last trip. I stood four on and four
off and I still haven't gotten used to getting my sleep in snatches. I hope to
get accustomed to it by the time we reach our destination. One nice thing
about this place is that they have beer. There is only one catch to it. They
serve only so much and first there first served. I came in last and didn't
get any. (Had to take time out to eat chow)

     The Captain wants me to go ashore for some provisions so will have to
finish this in a hurry. I think I will be able to write to you regularly
rather soon, at least for a while. We should be at our destination within a
month but you never can tell what might happen or who may change our plans.
I will be glad to get some place where we can dock for a few weeks rather
than a few days. I would like to see some of this country but I doubt if I
can get any time off. I am hoping for a leave in about three or four months.
That really isn't such a long time to wait if I can get back to the States
for a couple of weeks. All I can do now is hope and I am doing plenty of that.

     Have to get going now.

                                        Love to all,
                                             Tom
