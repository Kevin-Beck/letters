                                                  Feb. 3, 1943
Dear Mother,
     It happened this evening - I was transfered. I hated to leave the other
ship but I think I will learn more much quicker here. The skipper on this ship
is a full Lt. and he should at least know something about running it. I will
have to know a lot more before he will be satisfied.

     I found it quite difficult at times just standing around watching my old
skipper do things which I knew were wrong but I couldn't say a word. It is his
ship and he wanted to do as he pleased. He also wanted to learn the hard way,
experience, and refused advice. Anything I was responsible for I would agree to
his way and then do it mine. I really have no kick coming as we got along fine
to-gether.

[--- 1943-02-03_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     I am enclosing two money orders. I don't have any use for the money now
and I don't know what to tell you to do with it. I will let John figure that
out.

     I received a letter from George to day. He didn't have much to say except
semesters started and his schedule is fairly tough. I think he will be able to
handle it allright. He is no dumbbell.

     I have a lot of work to do so had better close now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
