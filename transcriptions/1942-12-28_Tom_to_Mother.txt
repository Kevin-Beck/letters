                                                  12/28/42
Dear Mother,
     I arrived a little late and safe. I logged in and nothing was said about
being late so I guess I am o.k. The crew left on the 22nd for Portsmouth and I
am to meet them there to-morrow. I rode down from Washington with some men in
the other training crew. They [crossed out: said] each crew received a 4 day
leave for Christmas. I was glad to hear that.

     My address will be different from now on. I am staying aboard this ship
just for the night. The commanding officer is staying ashore to-night &
offered me his bed.

     I haven't much else to say. I am a little tired - the train ride wasn't
very restful.

     It is just like Spring down here. I may have to peel my long underwear
off.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
