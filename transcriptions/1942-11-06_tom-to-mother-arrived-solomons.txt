[Printed letterhead: seal "U.S. NAVAL RESERVE MIDSHIPMEN'S SCHOOL", "NEW YORK, N.Y."]

                                                  [later hand: ? 12/18/42]
                                                  Friday
Dear Mother,
     We left Norfolk yesterday morning and arrived here last night. The trip
took 12 hours and wasn't very exciting. We hit some rough water and I almost
got sick. I slept most of the way on an improvised bed. There were at least
200 officers & enlisted men in one big room. The only chairs were camp stools
so I put two to-gether and managed to get some sleep.

     After we arrived we checked in and then ate our first meal since
breakfast. They haven't quite enough rooms for us so they have put cots in
the club rooms of the different building. There are 25 of us in this one
room. There isn't any thing for us to do yet. I have a watch to-morrow morning
and I guess I will miss the captain's inspection.

[--- 1942-11-06_tom-to-mother-arrived-solomons_p02.jpg ---]

     I have seen some of the barges at the dock and the big ones are really
nice looking ships. A lot of the fellows don't like it here but so far I have
no complaints. What most of them don't like is that we don't get any liberty,
and if we do there is no place to go. I think we will only be here a few
weeks so I can stand staying in. It is quite a change from the last few days
in Norfolk.

     You can send my clothes down as soon as possible. I won't need my blues
or whites for awhile so you can keep them. You can send the sweater and sox
Mrs. Mortimer gave me. I guess that is about all I can think of for now.

     My new address is:-

          U.S. Naval Amphibious training Base
          Soloman's, Maryland.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
