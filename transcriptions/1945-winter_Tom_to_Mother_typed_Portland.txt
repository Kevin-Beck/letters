DEAR MOTHER,

     AGAIN I HAVE THE DUTY AND I MUST ADMIT THAT I HAVE SPENT MORE ENJOYABLE
SATURDAY NIGHTS, AND ALSO WORSE ONES! SINCE I HAVE TO STAY ABOARD AND THIS
BEING A RATHER SLOW DAY I DID SOME WORK ON THE CHEV THIS AFTERNOON AND EVENING.
I AM STILL BOTHER WITH WATER IN THE GAS AND I DECIDED TO DO SOMETHING ABOUT IT.
THE LAST TIME I TOOK TO A GARAGE AND THE MECHANIC FIXED IT FOR AWHILE. SO
TONIGHT I TOOK THE CARBURETER AND FUEL PUMP OFF AND CLEANED THEM AND BLEW OUT
THE LINES. AFTER I PUT IT TOGETHER I WAS QUITE SURPRISED THE THE MOTER TURNED
OVER MUCH BETTER THAN BEFORE. PERHAPS I AM A MECHANIC, BUT NO DOUBT SOME PEOPLE
HAVE OTHER IDEAS ON THAT SUBJECT.

     BEFORE I FORGET IT MY TELEPHONE NUMBER IS PORTLAND 3-0411. SORRY I HAVEN'T
CALLED BEFORE THIS.

     MY WEEK HAS BEEN OCCUPIED WITH MOSTLY WITH MOVIES. SAW "THE FIGHTING LADY",
MRS. PARKINGTON", AND "GIRL RUSH". TRIED TO GET INTO SEE A BASKET-BALL GAME
LAST NIGHT BUT THERE WAS NO WAY TO REACH US BY 'PHONE AND LANC [Lane] HAD THE
DUTY AND WAS ON CALL. IF THE ICE DOESN'T MELT TOO MUCH BEFORE TOMORROW
AFTERNOON I THINK I WILL TRY SOME SKATING. IT IS FUN BUT MY LEGS CAN'T TAKE TOO
MUCH OF IT.

     NOT THAT I HAVE ANY SENSE NOR AM I BRAGGING BUT I HAVE PRACTICALLY QUIT
SMOKING. I HEARD TWO OFFICERS TALKING ABOUT QUITTING SMOKING AND THE BETS THEY
HAD ON IT. I DECIDED TO SEE IF I COULD QUIT WITH OUT ANY STRINGS. WENT TWO DAYS
WITH OUT ANY AND THE LAST TWO HAVE HAD ONE IN THE MORNING AND ONE IN THE
EVENING. I DID BUY SOME CIGARS AND WHILE I WAS WORKING ON THE CAR I CHEWED ONE
OF THEM. I DON'T FEEL ANY DIFFERENT BUT PERHAPS IF I KEEP IT UP FOR AWHILE I
MIGHT GAIN SOME WEIGHT. I AM MAKING NO PROMISES ABOUT THE LENGTH OF REFRAINING
AND ALWAYS CARRY A PACK JUST IN CASE I CHANGE MY MIND.

     MY NEW ROOMMATE IS GOING ON A TEN DAYS LEAVE STARTING MONDAY AND I WILL BE
BY MYSELF AGAIN. HE IS GOING TO KEEP ON LIVING WITH ME UNTIL HIS WIFE COMES BACK
OR HE GET ORDERS. EVERY ONE IS WORRYING ABOUT GETTING ORDERS BUT ME AND I QUIT
WORRING A COUPLE OF MONTHS AGO. I HAVE FOUND THAT IT DOESN'T PAY TO WORRY ABOUT
THE NEXT PLACE. THIS TOWN HASN'T CHANGED ANY BUT I AM NOT COMPLAINING. I AM
GETTING BY AND MY MEMORY ISN'T SO SHORT THAT I STILL DON'T BELIEVE THAT ANY DUTY
IN THE STATES IS GOOD DUTY.

     I HOPE YOU DON'T MIND MY TYPING LETTERS. IT ISN'T THAT I TYPE WELL BUT THAT
I SEEM TO WRITE MORE WHEN I USE THIS MACHINE. I HAVE ALSO HEARD COMPLAINTS ABOUT
MY SCRIBBLING. I HOPE EVERYONE IS FINE AND THAT YOU ARE GETTING THE SNOW
CLEARED. THE SNOW IS DISAPPEARING UP HERE THANKS TO THE SUN.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
