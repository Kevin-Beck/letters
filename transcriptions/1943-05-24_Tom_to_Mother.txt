                                                  May 24, 1943
Dear Mother,
     As I have said before there will be times when you won't hear from me for
several days and if a few days have elapsed between the last letter and this
one you can tell we have been underway again. Of course I could write while
underway but still couldn't mail it until we hit port. I have very little
spare time to write when underway. I am still standing the 12-4 watches which
I don't mind so much now.

     There is one thing you mentioned in one of your old letters just received
which I forgot to tell you about. Just before leaving I had Uncle Frank's gun
ready to take into him but failed to do so. My memory isn't so good any more
but I think the bolt for the gun should be in the front bedroom. I am very
sorry about not

[--- 430524pg2.jpg ---]

returning it and hope that it hasn't caused anyone any trouble. Uncle Frank
was so good to me by letting me use it. I might get him a Jap rifle in return.

     There is one subject which will usually take a full paragraph and
sometimes a whole page - Mail. I won't dwell on the subject long but just
mention that it will probably take a few weeks for it to catch up with me
again. It is one thing worth-while waiting for.

     I had a rather full day to-day and expect a busy one to-morrow. We pulled
in here at dark so I want to get a look at the place. It is 2130 so will
close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
