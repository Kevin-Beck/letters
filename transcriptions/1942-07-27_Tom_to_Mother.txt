                                                  27 July 1942
Dear Mother,
     I am on watch now and there isn't much going on at the present. I am in
charge of the 7th deck. It is tiresome work but I am lucky to-day. This is
the first week day it has rained and that we needed raincoats. Most of the
fellow got wet and I won't be out of the building to-day. I hope it clears up
for tomorrow.

     My first leave wasn't very exciting. There [crossed out: were] three of
us and we just bummed around. Saw a broadcast Saturday night and went to
radio-city on Sunday. Most of the people treated us swell although they
hadn't the slightest idea what we are. Most of them thought we were in the
merchant marine. I had fun and hope to be able to get out next week.

     What ever week-end you and Dad want to come up will be allright with me.
[crossed out: I was just] (I have been relieved and am back in my room where
I can use ink. I was writing this on the sly.) I was reading the rules and
regulations and they say visitors are
[line cut off at bottom of scan]

[--- 420727pg2.jpg ---]

whole regiment forms on the field and an officer goes through the ranks
inspecting the men. It might be considered colorful as every one is in full
dress. I only hope I am not on the tree when you come.

     Our watch system is run by the midshipman with a commissioned officer in
charge. It is divided into two section and each section alternate every four
hours. The "off section" has to stand by for work detail. I even hit that
lucky. I had to dust the officers desks and empty ash trays. I had a tough
time appearing to be busy because there wasn't any dust. The other section had
to clean the bulkheads on my deck. I am glad I didn't hit that section.

     It has been raining all day and I didn't leave the building. The other
fellows come in looking like soaked rats. The raincoats are good but very
heavy for this warm weather.

     While I was on work detail one of the fellows, Armagost, ask me where I
was from. I told him Pittsburgh and he wanted to know what parts. As usual I
told him about 50 miles north

[--- 420727pg3.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

at [?] Butler. He wanted to know more so I told him Karns City thinking he
never heard of it. I was mistakened - he is from Rimersburg. I don't remember
of ever seeing him but I think I have heard of the name.

     The last I have heard from the boy from Indiana he is in the Naval
Hospital with apendicitis. [crossed out] He is the second one from our deck
with it. I hope it is not catching.

     I don't think I want the Eagle. I haven't read a paper since I got here.
The only thing I read is the funnies on Sunday.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom

P.S. I got in touch with Bob Hines and he expects to be stationed here
beginning August 1. It will be nice seeing him again.
