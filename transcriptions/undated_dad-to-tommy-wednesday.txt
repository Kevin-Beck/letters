Letter: DAD (John A. Beck) to "Tommy"  - written TO Tom, not by him
Date: Undated - headed "Wednesday". Probably late January of Tom's college years
      at Penn State (c. 1937-1941): Dad mentions Tom's grades coming out, stopping
      at "State", and a summons to Philadelphia on "Feby 1st".

                                                  Wednesday
Dear Tommy -
     From what John told me over the phone last night I think he will be gone
before this reaches you. The Bank will accept the check if you endorse for
John and deposit.

     It just would not do to have a couple of young bloods down in New York
without an account to draw on, and especially after I had a chance to observe
their capacity for beer a couple of weeks ago.

     We will be disappointed in not seeing you - but hope you have a good time.

     It looks as though I will have to be in Phila on Feby 1st - got a summons
to

[--- undated_dad-to-tommy-wednesday_p02.jpg ---]

appear at a meeting there in connection with that oil investigation. I may be
able to stop of at State either going or coming home. If possible I want to be
around when we get your grades - and what a shout there will be if you pass.
And if you don't we just won't do any worrying about it - I'm sure it will work
out alright.

     You tell John I don't like his inference about spending time with me
instead of looking up "hotspots". Maybe the old man could show him some spots
in New York which he never even knew existed.

     Come home to see us as soon as you get a chance.
                                             Dad.
