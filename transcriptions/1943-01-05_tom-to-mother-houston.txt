                                                  Jan. 5, 1943
Dear Mother,
     We arrived here this morning about 10 hrs. late. Since my orders are to
report on the 7th I am going to wait until the 7th. I walked around downtown
here this afternoon and then slept for a couple of hours.

     I don't feel in the best of health to day. There was about 10 hours
layover at New Orleans and the boys were given liberty. I went along with
some of them and they were just about to much for me. There was a load of
recruits leaving in the car ahead of us and a lot of people to see them off.
The gang of sailors got in among

[--- 1943-01-05_tom-to-mother-houston_p02.jpg ---]

the crowd and I had a devil of a time getting them aboard. I think the boys
like New Orleans because they were a miserable sight this morning.

     The base here is small but very nice. The men's barracks were much better
than any other we have been to. A lot of the officers stay in town but I think
I will stay at the base, at least until I know my way around. I don't know yet
how long I will be here but will let you know as soon as possible.

     The reason I wired for money is that they closed my pay account before I
could get any money and I had to pay for my own fare. I don't know how long it
will be before I get payed again nor when I will get reimbursed. I didn't
want to run short.

     One thing that surprised me about Texas - there isn't a bar in the whole
state. The[y sell]

[--- 1943-01-05_tom-to-mother-houston_p03.jpg ---]

[Rice Hotel letterhead]

beer at a few places but any grog has to be purchased but the bottle. I can't
quite picture myself getting a bottle & coming to the room to drink it so will
stick to beer.

     It is after 7:00 so had better close & get some dinner. Will write again
to-morrow.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

P.S. I think this address will be good as long as I am here.
