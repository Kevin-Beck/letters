                                                  Wednesday Evening
                                                  Sept. 15, 1943
Hiya Sweetheart,
     Received your letter from Ocean City a few days ago and this is my first
chance to answer it. I was quite glad to hear that you finally took a
vacation. It is nice that you liked the resort and it sounded like you were
having an excellent time. No doubt the rest was good for you.

     Once before you mentioned considering moving to Butler for the winter. Is
that still your plan or are you also thinking of Florida. You would be wise to
do just as you please and don't [crossed out] let other people influence you.

[--- 1943-09-15_tom-to-mother-sweetheart_p02.jpg (marked "-2") ---]

     What I have to say now is just wishful thinking and for heavens sake
don't make any plans. There has been a change in the plans for our relief and
the whole thing has been dropped for the present. It may come into effect
again by the first of the year and that is what we will consider. Most men
that do get relief get new construction, like when I was in Houston and what
Don W. is doing now. Anyway, it will take a few months at some shipyard and if
it is down South you could keep house for me, probably will be a hotel room.
The invitation still goes for the North if you don't mind the cold weather.
It sums up to this - when I get home I expect to be in and near the states
for a few months and if you [crossed out: thing] think

[--- 1943-09-15_tom-to-mother-sweetheart_p03.jpg ---]

you would care for the life of a Navy wife every thing will be o.k. As I said
before it is wishful thinking.

     Your birthday party must have been swell. That is one other event I
regret missing. Don't start grabbing for a cane for another year or so. We
have a lot to do and can't be bother with a cane.

     Saw Geo. W. again but not for a few days. Hope he is still around so I can
visit him occasionally. He mentioned that when we get home we will have a
party with Isabel and the Dick Becks. They were all that he included.

     I will close now and save my usual unnecessary remarks for the next
letter.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck
