[Printed letterhead: eagle, shield and anchors, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

                                                  Sunday
                                                  1943 [sic]
Dear Dad,
     I can't give you any news now because I told Betty & the Johns every
thing. The only thing I regret about the trip is that I was in a bad mood. I
was plenty burned up because I couldn't get leave and was quite bitter last
night. This place really isn't as bad as I led them to believe.

     I have been transfered again and this time to [crossed out] LCI (158').
We were to leave to-morrow but I expect to be here most of next week. In this
ship there are 3 officers & 21 men. For once we are complete except for a
skipper. I am the executive officer and will be in charge until the

[--- page 2: 4211BCPg1.jpg ---]

skipper arrives. On the big ships the exec. practically runs the thing but on
the smaller boats the responsibility is evenly divided.

     I am sorry about the mix up on the clothes. A truck from here goes to
Washington every day and it brought them down. They weren't mailed.

     We have no table or desks in the barracks and it is very difficult to get
a comfortable writing position. Right now I am lying across the bunk using a
suitcase for a desk. The trouble with this set up is I have to reach down too
far.

     You can keep writing here until I send you another address. It will be
c/o P.O. N.Y.
     Will write soon,
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
