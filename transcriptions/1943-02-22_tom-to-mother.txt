                                                  Feb. 22, 1943
Dear Mother,
     Just finished a meal of beans. I haven't changed any and neither have
they. Except for the beans I have been eating a lot. The cook on board is a
good baker and I have been eating pie & cake. The meals are very good.

     Another change has been made in our address. Don't use Amphibious Force.
Use what is on the envelope.

     The main reason for this letter is that it may be the last one for awhile.
If you don't receive for a few weeks you will know that I am at sea again,
[crossed out: and that] I will write as soon as I can [find] any place to mail
a letter.

     There is one place I wish I could have seen before leaving the States -
the West Coast.

[--- 1943-02-22_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

I probably see it on the way back.
     It is time for the mail to go out so will have to close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
