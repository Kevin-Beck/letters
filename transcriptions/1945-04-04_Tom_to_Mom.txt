                                                  4 April
Dear Mom,
     I am still just sitting around doing nothing but trying to keep busy. I
have been taking an afternoon walk around the yard weather permitting. See a
movie about every night - taking in a USO show to-night. Haven't tried
Portsmouth for night life and don't intend to.

     Don't get your hopes too high. There is a possibility of my getting a
leave. I don't know when or for how long but will let you know in plenty of
time.

     My life of ease gets monotonous but I won't complain.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
