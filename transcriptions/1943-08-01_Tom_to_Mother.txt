                                                  Sunday
                                                  Aug. 1, 1943
Dear Mother,
     The boat is coming soon to take our mail so I will write just a few
lines. Received a few more letters which I will answer later.

     I am glad George likes the Navy. Of course he hadn't spent much time there
when he wrote. I am anxious to hear what he has to say after a few months of
it. Whether or [crossed out] not he realizes it, he could be in a lot poorer
organizations.

     I am waiting patiently for the story on the wedding. John really did get
a break getting Stephens furniture. Is he going to use the house too.

     This is a silly letter but I [j]ust wanted you to know I am still
[t]hinking of you.
