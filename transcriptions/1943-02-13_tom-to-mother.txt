                                                  Feb. 13, 1943
Dear Mother,
     From what I hear Air-Mail is so much faster that this will probably arrive
before my first letter. I guess it doesn't make much difference. From now on I
think I will send all letters Air Mail when ever possible or maybe V-mail.

     We have been kept quite busy on the ship and it isn't hard to work up a
good sweat. Last night was my first night ashore and it was very enjoyable. The
town is quite quaint or at least different. Almost every other door leads to a
bar and every place is jammed. It is unusual now but I think

[--- 1943-02-13_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

it would be a boring life to be here permanent. I am glad I am aboard ship and
not stationed on land.

     I have a lot more confindence in these ships now. After our trip I believe
that they can take quite a bit of punishment.

     I am sorry I can't write more but you understand how things are.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
