[Printed letterhead: eagle seal, "U.S. ARMY"]

                                   Pvt. William G. Jameson
                                   Co. F. 51st Engineers Combat Regt.
                                   Plattsburgh Barracks
                                   New York
                         Monday Eve
                           8:00
Dear Mrs. Beck.
     I received the can of Pop corn you sent to me to-day and it is very good.
I want to thank you for it and for keeping me in mind. I also got Janny's
cookies and Isabel's bullmatches [?] to-day, and received the post cards Isabel
sent me yesterday. I don't have time to write to all now, so please thank
Isabel for me. I shall never forget all the Becks and the good times I have
spent with them.

     The army has sent me up here where it is good and cold, so I'll just have
to make the best of it. I now have two weeks of basic training in and when I
get it all in, I hope to get in officer's training or Air-force. Tom Banks came
up with me, but I only see him once or twice a week. We have so much work to do
that we don't have much time to see each other. We have a show in

[--- undated_bill-jameson-to-mrs-beck_p02.jpg ---]

camp we can go to a night, also the Canteen, lots of beer.

     In the morning we have drill work outside and Classes in the day room. The
first classes were on First aid, but now, more in the line of engineering,
building bridges etc. The afternoons we take hikes in the woods and now
constructing a bridge. The best time in the Army is chow time, we have all the
meat, sugar, and coffee we want.

     Hope you are all well, and thanking you again for all things you have sent.
                                        Yours truly
                                             Bill
