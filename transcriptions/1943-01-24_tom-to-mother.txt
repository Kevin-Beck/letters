                                                  Jan. 24, 1943
Dear Mother,
     I am sorry I slacked off on writing - for a change I have been working.
Our ship was commissioned Friday and we leave to-morrow for Galveston. There
was, and still is, so much to do that everyone is traveling in circles. I
didn't get time to see either the McMahons or the O'Connors. I am sorry about
that. If we get any liberty in Galveston I am going to see Jimmy Eisler [?] &
the rest.

     Tuesday the temperature was down to 18° - by Saturday it was in the 80's.
I can't get used to it. If it stays warm I will [crossed out] my whites so will
you send both the suit & the shoes. Particularly, the shoes. I think I will get
them quicker if you send them here to be forwarded. Use the same address -
Brown [#345, inserted], etc.

[--- 1943-01-24_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     I have received a lot of mail and some presents. John Mc. send tobacco & a
pipe, Luke & Lena wash clothes, & John B. mits. Both Uncle Paul & Adam took off
enough time to write.

     John wrote about the lease and I am all for the plan he outlined. I can't
see why it won't work even tho' there will be so many partners. He also asked
about travelers checks and I don't think I will need them. The pay will be
regular and when it isn't I won't be where I need money. I am going to start
sending an allotment home. I don't know what to tell you to do with it but I
don't think I should carry it with me. We can decide that later.

     I will try to do better about writing from now on.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
