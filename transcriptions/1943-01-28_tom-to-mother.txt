                                                  Jan. 28, 1943
Dear Mother,
     It is just about time for taps so this will have to be short. We are
still doing a lot of work [crossed out] are still finding it interesting.

     At times the Engineering Officer will ask me some question about oil and
I catch myself pulling the same of stunt. Telling him [crossed out] a little I
do know and a lot more. I will have to quit that there because he believes me
and some day we will be without engines. It is really surprising what I have
pretended know and after I do it, find out that I did know something about
it.

[--- 1943-01-28_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     I don't want to show any partiallity but I had better tell Isabel "Happy
Birthday" now while I think of it. This will be the first one I have
remembered for a long time. I even forgot Jackie's which is one I should
remember.

     The other officers are ready for bed and I have to shave to-night so will
close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

USS L.C.I.(L) 345
c/o Postmaster
New York, N.Y.
