Brooklyn, New York City]

                                                  Wed. Afternoon
My dear Tommy -
     I got your letter today, also Johnny's, but it was too late for me to get
you the baseball suit for I had already gotten you something. It is something
used in a baseball game. We can get you a suit nearer home and then if it
don't fit it won't be so much trouble to exchange it. We can get a glove for
Johnny at home too. I know you'll like the present I am bringing you.

     If you look in mother's bedroom when you wake up Saturday morning I'll bet
you will be surprised.

[--- page 2: from dad undated2.jpg ---]

     Was out to play golf again today and I had the nicest blue eyed little boy
for a caddy - he reminded me a lot of you. He followed us around for a long time
without saying anything, and finally asked if he couldn't carry my clubs. Said
he wanted to learn how to be a caddy. I asked him if he had ever tried it before
and he said he did but wasn't very good - just like you would say. I let him
caddy for me and showed him how to do it, and when we finished I gave him 50¢.
My but he was tickled. Most of the caddies are dirty looking foreigners and you
don't like to have them around you.

     Well Tommy, daddy will be home pretty soon and surely will be glad to play
with his little boys again. I am tickled that you are doing so well with your
papers.
                                        From your
                                             Daddy.
