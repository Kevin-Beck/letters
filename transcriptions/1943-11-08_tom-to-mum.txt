                                                  Nov. 8 - 43
Dear Mum,
     There isn't much to say & I haven't much time so this will be short.

     I received the first package and thanks a million. I haven't had time to
read it but I know it will be good. Again thanks.

     I am sorry to say the pictures never arrived and I think they have had
plenty of time by now. It is too bad because I wanted to see them.

     The Air Mail is still not coming through as it should. Perhaps it will be
back on schedule in a few weeks.

     This is a damn poor letter but the best I can do at the present.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck
