                                                  Feb. 18, 1943
Dear Mother,

     Just a short note to let you know I am still kicking around. There has
been no change- just the same old routine. We are still working and sweating.
I still haven't tried to acquire a tan yet, but will venture forth one of
these days.

     I saw Chet Beard a few minutes ago. He hasn't change much. Complains a
little more than he used to. When I left him he was arguing with a Lieutenant
and I think he will lose the arguement. He was married while he was waiting
for his ship in Phidelphia. He also said that Johnson and a few other of the
John Jay boys came with him. I haven't seen any of them as yet.

     It is beginning to seem like ages since I have heard from home. I know it
isn't your fault but just the same I have been wondering how all of you are. I
suppose Jackie is getting cuiter and wilder every day. If you are having
typical February weather I imagine it is cold with plenty of snow. How are you
making out with the oil being rationed? Probably keeping pretty close to the
back living room or are you practicing bundling.

     You know that I never did hear the story about Al Wiengard leaving.
Perhaps the mail won't carry it if you wrote it verbatem.

     I will have to get back to work.

                                        Love,
                                        [signature below edge of scan]
