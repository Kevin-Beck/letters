                                                  25 Dec. 1943
Dear Olive & Bob,
     We had an excellent turkey dinner to-day with fruit cake for dessert. The
cake arrived in fine condition and tasted wonderful. Eating food that isn't
dehydrated is really a rare treat. You can see that your gift was appreciated
and I really do mean "Thank You."

     I believe that for most of the men out here being away at Christmas time
wasn't as tough as they thought it would be. The folks back home have been
swell. Each man has received many packages and we all enjoy them so much.

     There is very little to say about myself. I still enjoy good health and I
miss [not, inserted] being at home. I hope the time isn't too far in the
future when I will be home again.

     I hope you have a very successful New Year.
                                        Sincerely,
                                             Tom

[Written vertically in left margin:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck

[--- 1943-12-25_tom-to-olive-and-bob-pelkey_p02.jpg (typed on the back of an air-mail envelope) ---]

Dear Elva:   We are sending you Tommy's letter - please dear don't think its
because we sent a small package but Bob thought you might like to see his
writing and it was on Xmas day too. Hope you got through the holidays O.K. We
were very quite and didn't do any celebrating New Year's eve either account of
everyone feeling so low after having "FLU".
     When you write next time please give me Spitzers correct mailing address
because Bob and I want to send them a box of California fruits. I did write
them after our lovely pinic but thought for a box I should have a confirmation
of their address. Also what is Dr. Mortimer's initial and address?
     I hope you are all O.K. and I know busy as ever,
                                        Love to all,
                                             Olive     1/5/44
