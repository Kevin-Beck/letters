                                                  7-15-42
Dear Adam,
     I received your letter and one from Sis to-day and I really appreciated
both. I will be quite busy for the next few days so I will consider this an
answer to both. They cut our course in half and we are having finals the end
of this week. The amount of work they are throwing at us I think the only
thing they cut is the time. The work isn't real tough but we are going so
fast that it is difficult to grasp everything.

     So far I haven't any complaints. It is tough getting use to a regimented
life and to behave like gentlemen all the time but I suppose it is just as
tough for the rest. Our bounds are so small that it is worse than

[--- 1942-07-15_tom-to-adam-moser_p02.jpg ---]

being up in the mountains at State College. At least we could drink beer
there.

     I think I am rather fortunate rooming near a bunch of good fellows. All
the fellows within a few doors of me have so far been O.K. I have seen a few
guys floating around that I wouldn't want to be seen with any place. There
are fellows here from almost every state, the majority seems to be from the
South.

     I went to a USO dance Saturday night and if what I saw was a cross
section of city girls, I'll stick to the corn-fed ones.

     I will have to start studing soon so I will call this a letter. Don't be
afraid to write often.

                                             Tom
