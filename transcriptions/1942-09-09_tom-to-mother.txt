[Printed letterhead (blue): eagle, shield and anchors, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

                                                  9 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     I quite imagine you had a swell time at the Lake over the holiday. I
envied the picture you described, especially the rest. I suppose my quiet
days are over for a while. Labor Day was just like July 4th, just another day.

     Our graduation schedule was finally posted. We have finals the 15th & 16th
and graduation the 21st. The reason we get a few days off is to buy uniforms
etc. and for the Navy to decide if they want us. On the same schedule are the
plans for the next class at John Jay. Those poor fellows get a tough break -
their first term finals are on Dec 31 & Jan. 1. Incidently there was an
announcement that any Jew could get permission to go home for their holidays.
The feeling against them is rapidly rising among the white men here.
Something is brought

[--- 1942-09-09_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

up about them in every bull session.

     We get a break next Saturday. Instead of having review here in the
morning we are having it at the All-Star game. I was planing on going to the
game anyway.

     My roommate was caught in the last bilge. I was sorry to see him go. I
had a feeling he would get it sooner or later and I tried to get him to work
a little harder but he wouldn't stick to the books very long. He was a good
fellow and I think better officer material than some that stayed.

     An officer was here last night interviewing for bomb disposal school and
he didn't interest me. Quite a few of the fellows made application [b]ut I
think I have had enough school w[or]k for awhile.

     I must go on watch soon [so] had better close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
