                                                  ? 11/20/42
                                                  Friday 1942
Dear Dad,
     I had quite a jolt when I read Mother's letter but feel better now after
talking to you. I am so happy to know that you're much better and I do want
you to take it easy. Don't just lay off for a few weeks and then go back. If
you don't want to retire at least try semi-retirement (if there is such a
thing).

     I guess I didn't make it very clear how things are here. When I said I
would be here two weeks it was only a guess, no one knows. We are on 24 hours
notice. Usually they tell a man the night before he is to leave the next
morning on the 0600 boat. The last batch that left some of the officers
weren't notified until 2 or 3 in the morning. That is the reason we aren't
given any liberty.

[--- 421120pg2.jpg ---]

     There is a general shake up of officers now and I was replaced by a
gunnery officer. They gave me another crew but I am the only officer and there
is no crew. The crews are numbered & assigned chronologically and the crews in
the 4030's aren't filled yet. My new number is 4074. I don't know what it
means. I may be transfered any day or be here for weeks.

     I like this place but I don't know why. Men come and go so fast that the
administration can't keep up and just go around in a circle. I think they
will get organized soon. We haven't any recreational facilities and spend all
our extra time in our room. There are eight of us in this room and five are
from John Jay. All good boys.

     I played some Black Jack last night and got nicked for two bucks. That
will satisfy my gambling urge for awhile.

     Take care of yourself.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
