                                                  27 Nov. 43
Dear Mum,
     There is nothing startling occurring around here but I haven't written
for a few days. I did receive a letter from you yesterday. Only one package
has come through so far.

     There is a good movie to-night, Bob Hope, and there is also a game of
skill being formed. I think I will take in the game since pay day is coming
soon. I hope I have better luck to-night than the last time.

     I will enclose a request for Ernie Pyle's book in a future letter. I also
intend to write to John soon about income tax, etc.

[--- 431127pg2.jpg ---]

     We had a good Thanksgiving dinner and I really stuffed myself. We had
turkey, oyster stew, stuffing, olives, mashed potatoes, and cranberries, which
are [crossed out: rare] and still don't like. It was a good meal and tho' not
eaten under the best circumstances I think we all have a lot to be thankful
for. There are many much worse off than I am.

     I hope you enjoyed you Thanksgiving whether spent at Lewisburg, Butler, or
home. I was thinking of you all at that time especially.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] [En]s. Thos. F. Beck
