                                                  May 17, 1943
Dear Mother,
     Being a very busy person and with 10 mens work to do immediately, I am
going to take time off to write. There is no urgent need for a letter but I
just happen to be in the mood.

     As you can plainly see I found the check. I had to go through all the
letters I had saved and then remembered where I put it. My [crossed out:
memory] still isn't the best. I saved only the ones from the family plus a few
others. While reading Marge's letters it seemed strange for her to be talking
of dances, etc. She must really be growing up.

     The life here isn't always routine. Yesterday, while unloading the
personal clothing that we are sending home, I went through hell. I was to see
that the clothes were taken care of and it was a tough job. I had a difficult
time getting a boat to get ashore and when I did get ashore I had a more
difficult

[--- 1943-05-17_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

time finding the right storehouse. When I did find it they wouldn't take
cardboard boxes so I had to send them back to the ships. But, and this was
what topped off everything, while unloading I dropped a box overboard. You
probably have guessed whose it was - the Captain's. Knowing the Captain has a
temper, I sent his clothes back with a Seaman and I went to the Club. By the
time I got back he had cooled off. He even spoke to me. I understand he spoke
to the poor Seaman too.

     I am not complaining now, but just remarking that I haven't received a
letter from you for a few days. I really expect one to-day. If not to-day,
then to-morrow or the day after. I know there is more than one on the way.

     I am anxious to hear what the Clinic said about your eyes. Betty said
your health is excellent, which certainly wonderful news. I do hope they can
help you eyes because I know they did bother you considerably. Your keep your
health in good condition and I will attempt the same with mine and then when
I get home we both

[--- 1943-05-17_tom-to-mother_p03.jpg ---]

can lead a wild life for awhile.
     As per usual when I want to finish a letter I end up at the top of a new
page and then have a hard time filling out the rest. It will be different this
time and will close now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
