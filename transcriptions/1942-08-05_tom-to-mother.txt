                                                  5 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     My visit with Isabel was nice but very short. It was a good thing Janny
mentioned her coming, otherwise we might have had some difficulty making
connections. I thought she was coming over the weekend. This was just another
day for us but I imagine Isabel enjoyed it.

     Your plans to come here might meet complications at this end. The watch
bill is all messed up and is not going in alphabetical order now. I had a
Sunday watch the first week-end and shouldn't get another one for [crossed
out: five or six weeks] about 10 weeks or maybe not at all. If you let me know
a week or two inadvance when you are coming I may know more then. Almost all
posts on the Sunday watch are on duty from 1700 Saturday until 1700 Sunday
and are not permitted to leave the ship.

     I was just called to the post office and received the camra. Thanks a lot.

[--- 1942-08-05_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

We have to wear white shirts with detachable collars when we wear our blues
so if there are any shirts at home not in use will you please send them. I
told Isabel about this.

     We don't have much idea what is in store for us in the future and I hear
a lot of rumours. The lastest one is that our graduation is October 2. That
seems to be cutting down quite a bit. Some new instructors arrived who have
received a commission after 1 months training. Judging them I think the Navy
is hard up for officers.

     We have another pay day soon. My first pay was $4.70. The deductions
were high. I hope this pay is better.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
