                                                  Oct. 9-43
Dear John & Fran,
     Received your letter, note, & picture all in one day. For some unknown
reason your letter, along with Isabel's, was held up about a week. Anyway, I
was very glad to hear from you. That was the first picture of the wedding I
have received. I really enjoy it so thanks a lot. You know I am after all the
pictures, mostly the family, I can get so send along any spares you have. I
have bragged of what a beautiful sister-in-law I have but maybe a single
picture wouldn't be proper.

     I am anxious to see your home now that it is all fixed up. It must take a
lot of planning to fix a house

[--- 431008pg2.jpg ---]

when you can't use either rugs or curtains. John must be a bit pessimistic
about the oil future to be building a coal cellar.

     You will have to break that resolution about inviting guests for
breakfast because I am looking forward to some nice light waffles. Of course I
neither stay up late nor get up early so my breakfast will be around noon. My
breakfast now consists of either powder eggs or pancakes (collision mats)
which would definately help the shoe situation back home.

     You trip to New York sounded nice even the races. No doubt your last trip
to the convention was also a lot of fun. Personally, I think

[--- 431008pg3.jpg ---]

New York is a grand place and some day I plan to spend a week or so there
hitting the high spots. Shore duty there would really be something.

     There is nothing new out here. It is the same routine every day which
gets very tiresome. There is so much scuttlebutt about our relief that I
believe very little I hear. It makes good conversation, however.

     Write when you find time and I will see what I can do about answering.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Ens Thos. F. Beck
