                                                  1/8/43
Dear Mother,
     I spent the last couple of days looking over the town and it is really a
nice place. I [crossed out] reported to the base yesterday and spent most of
to-day checking papers. There is a lot of paper work connected with equiping a
ship. I think we will be here for about two more weeks.

     We have our choice of either staying here or [crossed out] in town. The
base is 18 miles outside. It is much cheaper here at the base and pay day is a
long way off so I will stay here.

     I am finally going to get some laundry [crossed out: done]. It has been a
long time since my clothes have been real clean. I also bought another khaki
uniform.

[--- 430108pg2.jpg ---]

You can forget about the whites for awhile. It is actually cold down here. It
also has rained every day which is not what I expected from the South. I
suppose I expected too much.

     I have a muster in a few minutes so will close.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
