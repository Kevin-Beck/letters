                                                  Nov. 1942
                                                  Tuesday
Dear Dad,
     I have heard that this place is like a pig pen when it rains and now I
know for sure. It has rained the last two days and there is mud every where.
If possible, it must have been twice as bad before the sidewalks were put in.
I bought a pair of galoshes, not only for here but for when I get aboard. I
understand that the steel decks don't offer much heat.

     I guess my embarkation was a false alarm. I was scheduled to go Monday but
now I haven't any idea. They took seven of my crew away from me and I hope
they replace them soon. I expect to be called any day and I hope it is soon.

     I think the holiday leaves will be split with 1/2 the crew getting

[--- 1942-11-24_tom-to-dad_p02.jpg ---]

home at Christmas, the other 1/2 at New Years. That is if we get any leave.

     Chet came in again to-night and came up for a shower. He likes his work
and is anxious to get his own ship. The more I talk to him the more I want to
get out of here.

     I understand that the men that flunk out of John Jay now are kept in the
Navy as Seamen 2nd class. That's quite a let down.

     I can't even think of enough news to fill this page so had better close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

Happy Thanksgiving
