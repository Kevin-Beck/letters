                                                  "Moore Heights"
                                                  Wednesday.
Dear Folks:- I don't believe there is enough news to fill a letter but will at
least send you a short one.
     We are having very cool weather in the shade but boiling in the sun.
     I haven't seen the Fairview family since you left. Saw Johnnie at church
and told him I would like to hear his speech but he didn't get around to it.
     I went to Cavendish Club yesterday and think out of 16, I was next to
lowest. I think I will take up Bingo. We had a lovely time and a nice lunch.
     I was kinda tickled when

[--- 4505mothpg2.jpg ---]

I went in to see Bonnie she grabbed me and kissed me. I don't know whether I
am so motherly or just kissable and irresistable.
     Fred was out last night and put in my fixtures. Like two of them - am not
sure about the other one. 66 2/3 % is a pretty good batting average. Fred was
disgusted the other night. So much red tape working for the gov't. and his
helpers are none too good.
     Joan called yesterday and brought my clock. Smitty was here with my
insurance policy. Corbett black topped the driveway. I am gradually getting
things done. What has helped, I have found a hammer - also Irene Deets.
     Janny may be here Sunday.
                                        Love - Mother
