                                                  January 2, 1943
Dear Mother,
     I am sorry not to have written you sooner, but I have been very busy. We
are leaving to-night and it has been quite a job getting ready. The other two
officers arrived this morning & left this afternoon. I offered to ride with
[the] crew so they can go their own way.
     I will let you know what my address is as soon as possible. You might get
my white suit & shoes ready to ship. I am going to keep my heavy clothes with
me in case we get north again.

     New Years Eve was very dull. It was the only night I went out and then
didn't do anything. I went to the officers club at the Navy Yard. I was
waiting for a lot of noise at midnight. I waited until 10 minutes after and
nothing happened so I came back. This is the most unfriendly town I have
[crossed out] been in.

[--- 1943-01-02_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     They closed my pay account here before I could get paid. I think I have
enough money to get to Houston but if I don't any there for awhile you will
probably get a wire.

     I haven't received any mail yet but I suppose it will eventually get
here. The only Christmas mail received was Bill Jamison's & Luke & Lena [?].
     That is all for now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
