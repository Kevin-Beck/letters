                                                  Sept. 9 - 43
Dear Mom,
     It has only been a couple of days since I last wrote and the intervening
days have produced nothing new. I am so accustomed to say that that it is
automatic and this time it isn't quite true.

     Last evening just as I was sitting down to a good old fashion Navy meal -
beans, I had a visitor. I didn't recognize the man at first but I knew that
there was only one officer out here that I knew, that is outside of the
Flotilla. It was Geo. W. He has either grown at lot or I had forgotten how
large he was.

[--- 1943-09-09_tom-to-mom_p02.jpg ---]

I know he didn't gain any after eating our beans. We talked for awhile and then
decided to go to the movies - he had to take that boat back to the beach any
way. That boat never stopped but he caught a later one. I was supposed to meet
him to-day but the boat never showed up. I went to the movies to-night in hopes
of seeing him but no luck. I will try again to-morrow. In case Isabel is
interested, he asked how she was.

     The movie to-night was excellent. It wasn't a top ranking one but [it]
was too bad. The only thing I objected to was that the sound machine didn't
work all the time. Twice I heard about

[--- 1943-09-09_tom-to-mom_p03.jpg (marked "-3") ---]

five word. It was just like an old silent job only it didn't have the
conversations cluttering up the screen. The short was a musical and also silent
except for the last song which was a South Sea Island hula - beautiful women,
etc. It is quite ironical.

     Of course the main topic of conversation now is Italy's action. It
naturally brought new hopes to us as we all think that her surrender will
shorten the war. Italy's step and the progress in New Guinea (sp) has put us
all in good spirits.

     A little over a week ago a trainee officer came aboard. He is training for
the LCI's but we don't know whose relief he is. I am fairly

[--- 1943-09-09_tom-to-mom_p04.jpg ---]

sure he isn't mine. His name is Jim Hoffman and comes from Cleveland. Otherwise
I know very little about him.

     Your mail hasn't been coming through lately but expect it any day. Had a
letter from Florella and she mentioned some wedding pictures you were planning
to send. I imagine they are in the mail and will arrive soon.

     We had a discussion the other night about proof reading our own letters.
One said if a letter is worthwhile the time it should be done properly. So for
once I reread my letter to you and have decided you have to put up with a lot
when you read them. I think they are very dull and refuse to read any more.
Will leave that up to you.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck
