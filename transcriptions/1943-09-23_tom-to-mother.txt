                                                  Sept. 23-43
Dear Mother,
     The mail situation is still fouled up so it is hard to write. I haven't
any thing definate in mind now so shall start and come what may.

     Received a card from Luke & Lena to-day marked the 12th [crossed out] that
is good time for deliveries out here. Also heard some mail was lost which may
explain the slim mail calls.

     I have always said that I didn't need any thing out here. I have changed
my mind. My slippers are finally giving out and I can't get any here. So if
you will please send me a pair, preferably leather, size 10 1/2. We will
consider it Christmas.

     Some of the men went to a show to-night - put on by the

[--- 1943-09-23_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

Army. The reports are quite favorable and if it is given to-morrow night I
think I will attend. It will be something different.

     You can't guess what I had to-day. Went ashore and discovered the canteen
was selling ice-cream. It was the first I had for a long time. I only had two
cups of it - if the line hadn't been so long I think I would have eaten much
more - no doubt too much. It really tasted swell.

     Sorry the letter is so short but I can't help it. Anyway I am still
thinking of you and am letting you know I am still ok.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

Tell Marge I received a letter from her friend Thos. Lipinski.

[Written at bottom left:] Ens Thos. F. Beck
