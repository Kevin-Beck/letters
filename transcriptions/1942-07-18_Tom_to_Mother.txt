                                                  7/18/42
Dear Mother,
     When the Navy gets tough, they get tough. I just finished my last exam. I
thought math would be the hardest but it wasn't. I didn't think very much of
the others. It seemed to me they asked too many minor things but I guess
everything is important. I think my average will be between 75 & 80, which
is fair.

     They gave us a test when we first came and are now interviewing the
lower 50%. I haven't had an interview as yet.

     If I got through allright so far, and I think I did, I will start school
Monday as a Midshipman. I hope they don't condense that course as much as
they did this one.

     There is another dance to-night and I think I will try it again. Irving

[--- 420718pg2.jpg ---]

Berlin & Sigmund Romberg are to be there to entertain us.

     I am eating more now and am eating cake, cookies, & pie. Don't think
that isn't a hint.

     I sent Dick & Betty a telegram to-day. I am sorry I couldn't be there. I
don't know whether or not Dick is better being married or in the V-7.

     I was called away for a few hours and then went to the dance. I just got
back and am plenty tired.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom

P.S. Please don't say much about what I tell you the school, courses, etc.
They asked us not to say anything.
