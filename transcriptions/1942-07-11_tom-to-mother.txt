                                                  7-11-42
Dear Mother,
     I finally received my physical exam and passed all right. It was just a
check-up and not very tough. I don't know of anyone who has flunked it.

     We had a dance last night at the Prairie State sponsored by the Navy
League and the U.S.O. We had to march about 20 blocks to get there and then
fight for a dance. The ratio was about 3 [?] boys per girl. One of the fellows
told me how he stuck his roommate with a girl who couldn't dance. It wasn't
long until the fellow came up to me and ask me to entertain this friend while
he went to the head. He was gone before I could answer him and never did come
back. It was the same girl and I was afraid to try to

[--- 1942-07-11_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

dance so I spent about two hours talking. No one would come to my rescue. It
wasn't so bad and I imagine I will go back next week if possible.

     Life is quite routine now - classes, drill, study, eat, and sleep. The
is a roumor around that the indoctrination period will only be from 2-3 weeks.
Then we become midshipmen.

     I met Geo. Wagenhorst the other day. He straightened me out on a few
things and offered to help me anytime. I probably will need him more later
than now.

     I don't like these Sunday mornings of getting up early and sitting around
the rest of the morning with nothing to do. I could study but the idea doesn't
appeal to me at this time.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom

I just left John so this will be old news.
