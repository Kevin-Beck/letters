                                                  ? 11/18/42
                                                  Wednesday
                                                  1942
Dear Dad,
     So far to-day has been wonderful - I received a letter from you & Betty,
a package from Mother, a new crew, and a month's pay.

     I don't know what I can do about seeing Betty & John. I think I can get
leave from 5 to 10 Saturday and perhaps a little more but that won't give me
time to go any place. They would have to come down & meet me either at the
base or in Solomons & that is quite a ways from Washington. I don't know
whether or not it will be worth it for them.

     My new assignment is only temporary until new officers arrive. It will be
practice for me and keep the crew to-gether. I will try to explain what I am
doing here and it probably won't be very coherent. When I have a crew I muster
them 3 times a day - morning, noon, & evening - and

[--- 420716pg2.jpg ---]

march them to class. Certain days I march them to mess. If they have any
troubles, I listen to them and then pass them on to the senior officer.
As for my own classes last week I started in Elementary Navigation which was
too elementary and I tried Communications which was a brush-up on blinker &
semaphore. This week is being spent on enciphering & deciphering messages. I
admit it is a waste of time but there are so many specialists here that have
just been commissioned and have no training. The Columbia boys surprised them
with a little sea knowledge and we are still on the old schedule.

     As for practical work we get none here. There are three different size
ships, LCT, LCI, & LST in order of size. I have been on the LCT, & saw a few
LCI but couldn't get aboard. I am [crossed out: on] assigned a LST but the
harbor here is to small for them so closest I have been to them is a look at
the blue prints.

[--- 420716pg3.jpg ---]

     One thing you must remember is that the Amphibs is new & that the base is
new. Of course it is not as well organized as it could be but some new
officers are taking charge and I expect a change for the better. Another thing
the Navy is full of Red tape & customs which they hate to give up. I think
there is too damn many politicians in or influencing the Navy for it to
really function well.

     The scuttlebutt has it that quite a few orders are coming through to-night
which means some crews are leaving but I don't think I will be included. I
also heard that our base is being transferred to the West Coast. I guess the
Yanks are doing allright in Africa.

     I am glad to hear you are feeling better and every thing is fine. Please
do take care of yourself so it won't happen again.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
