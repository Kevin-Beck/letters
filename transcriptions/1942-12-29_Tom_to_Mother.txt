                                                  12/29/42
Dear Mother,
     I guess my being late didn't cause any uproar. I said nothing about it and
neither did any one else. I arrived here just in time to say good-bye to Baker
& Toben [?]. Both left for their ship. I am to leave soon but can't say where.
I am not clapping my hands because I am not going into the heart of it.

     The crew is full now and all seem to be good men. All we need now are
some officers.

     I took my heavy underwear off to-day and was still warm in just my khaki.
It is certainly a great change.

     While I was waiting for a taxi in front of the station I had a strange
conversation with an old lady. She said she was 84 years old & a true
Virginian. Her ancestors go back to early England but the more modern one was

[--- 421229pg2.jpg ---]

Patrick Henry. She has traveled world-wide but likes Florida the best. Spent a
few years in China & Japan - likes the Chinks, but not the Japs. Her husband
was a minister and she has her own ideas about religion. The conversation also
included her courtship, ideas about young people, & marriage. She was quite
interesting - I talked to her for about an hour. If I had more time here I
think I would go to visit her.

     That is all except be sure to put the crew number (3081) on all letters.
I think I will get mail faster that way.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
