                                                  1/11/43
Dear Mother,
     Received a letter [to-day, inserted] from both you and George. I gave you
the wrong address but they eventually caught up with me. It is strange we
weren't supposed to tell where we were going and now we not only give the name
of the town but also the company.

     This is really a good town. The people are very friendly. In fact they
seem to treat the sailors better than the soldiers. On Saturday night the town
was filled with service men mostly air cadets from Ellington Field. I couldn't
get a hotel room at 6:00 in the evening. I was fortunate in meet a kid here
that works nights so he gave me his bed a the "Y." I met this fellow the first
night I was here and he not only introduced me to his girl friend but also got
me a date with her. She is a nice kid and I have fun dating her most of the
time. It could be her name that interests me. It's

[--- 430111pg2.jpg ---]

Ophelia - I thought names like that exists only in books.

     While I was bouncing from crew to crew at Solomons one of my commanding
officers home was in Texas. I met the man at Paradise Creek and the morning of
the day I left I mentioned to him about coming to Houston. He said he had a
brother-in-law here & insisted that I look him up. I called the other day and
they insisted on my coming out to dinner some evening. They promised a good
meal so I think I will take them up on it. I will look up the people you
mentioned. I think I will leave here in a week and will probably go to
Galveston for awhile.

     One of the new members of the crew was at Africa and he said one of the
officers in his group was a Glenn Scott. From the description I am fairly sure
he is the one from Oil City.

     Don't be surprised if you get another telegram.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
