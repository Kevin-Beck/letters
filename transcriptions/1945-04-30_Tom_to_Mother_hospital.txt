                                                  30 April 1945
Dear Mother,
     I haven't been doing any thing but taking it easy. The doctors haven't
said a thing except that there are some more tests to take. I have no hopes of
getting out of here soon because there are others that have been in for six
weeks and more. Of course they know why they are here and as yet I am not sure.

     Lane stopped in Saturday for a few hours on her way to Newark. She had
some trouble getting transportation but imagine she is on her way by now. I
don't know how long she has to stay on the West Coast before taking off.

     Haven't heard from you for awhile.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
