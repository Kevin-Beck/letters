                                                  7/23/42
Dear Mother,
     Yesterday we were shot with typhoid serum, vaccinated, and some other
scratch on our arms. It sounded funny when the instructors said that if any
one left the room to pass out for someone to go with him. Quite a few of the
boys did pass out and more are laid up to-day. The way my arm felt yesterday
I didn't think I would be able to use it for a week. It still is sore but not
very noticable.

     Also we were sworn in as midshipmen yesterday. It was mass production -
800 at one time. We were also issued our blue uniforms. We get to wear our
white cap covers and white shirts - stiff collars are required. My uniform
does not fit even tho it is supposed to be

[--- 1942-07-23_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

tailor made. I have to wear it in order to get out of bounds for the week
end. I think I will go down town Saturday night and possibly a ballgame
Sunday.

     The article of the wedding was interesting but they didn't mention all
the clothes the bride took on the honeymoon.

     One thing I don't like about this place is the airplanes flying overhead.
Right now I can see 4 transports going by. There are a lot of Army planes
around here - everything from pursuits to the Flying Fortresses. I am trying
to break myself of the habit of looking up everytime one goes over. The
officers don't go for it.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
