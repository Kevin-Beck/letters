                                                  [later hand: ? 12/21/42]
                                                  Monday
Dear Mother,
     There is a Lt. here that was in the last war and has been stationed for a
few weeks and he has been telling some tall yarns. We have been in stitches
all evening. I have noticed a lot of the boys wearing hunting knifes & he said
some officer gave a pep talk that we were commandos and the kids bought of
the local hardware.

     I was over to the city last night. Started for the movie but changed my
mind when I found out it was a big dinning room with a screen at one end. I
stopped at the local bar and it was quaint. A little room back of the hotel
and it was filled with officers & enlisted men with a few civilians in a
corner the the center taken up with slot machines. There also was a nigger
playing

[--- 421221pg2.jpg ---]

the piano all night. That bartender has a mint.

     We were supposed to get some more men in our crew to-day but the only
change was we lost our only senior officer. The other two men hade watch and
I had to take the crew on a hike. I march them to the gate then just followed
them for a couple of miles to some pop stand then came back. I was afraid my
voice would crack giving commands but all it did was get a little high. The
work holds much more interest since I am working with a crew even if there are
only ten.

     Navigation classes started to-day and we were put in elementary classes.
The instructor felt rather foolish when someone told him we just finished a 3
months course. Tomorrow we start on some new tables.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom   [signature visible in second scan 430130Pg2]
