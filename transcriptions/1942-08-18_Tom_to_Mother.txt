                                                  18 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     It is hard to express just how much I enjoyed your visit. I had a grand
time all week-end. The only thing wrong was that it was too short. I got
soaked coming in from the subway and am just about over the cold I got.

     I was on watch again to-day. It was the telephone watch which is [not,
inserted] hard work but very tiring. One man called and asked to be connected
to some room. I was going to tell him that the fellow was in his private bath
and couldn't be disturbed, but the fellow was in class and I took the message.

     It was announced here that a second front was started and we all trying
to get the details. When we got a paper it was just a commando raid. I was
hoping that the dirty work would be over before I

[--- 420818pg2.jpg ---]

got there.
     We had an evacuation to-day. About 16 of the fellows left. We were also
informed that between 50 & 60 would follow them in a few weeks. That is after
the first term exams. From then on there will be about 15 bilged. It seems to
me that after each bilge they tighten their grip on us. Even navigation is
getting hard now.

     While I was dusting the officers desks I heard the commander say that
graduation will be October 13. That should be just about definite. I hope I
am here to participate.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
