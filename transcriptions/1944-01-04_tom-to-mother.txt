                                                  4 January 1944
Dear Mother,
     I guess the system of numbering letters isn't working out so well. I have
the envelopes numbered in advance but now I have discovered that I have been
taking them from the bottom and from the top. Rather fouled up at this point.

     I started to write to you on Sunday and then got side-tracked so I hope I
can finish this to-night.

     It is strange what some people will do. I went to the movies a few nights
ago. In the middle of the picture there was a very heavy mist and I got
soaked. Nevertheless, I sat through just to see a

[--- 1944-01-04_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

class B picture. I guess I am changing somewhat.

     I received a Time last night and I don't know who is sending it. I
suggested the idea to several people and would like to know just who agreed
with me that it is a good thing.

     Aunt Bess sent a nice book "Burma Doctor." Have read only part of it and
it is good. I have a lot of books to read now. Speaking of presents I have
some more troubles now. I am not sure who all I have written to thanking for
the presents so I suppose some will get two letters.

     I just remembered that tomorrow is another big day for the Becks. Betty's
and Mincy's [?] birthday.

[--- 1944-01-04_tom-to-mother_p03.jpg ---]

I still remember Betty's 21st. That doesn't sound so good, she isn't that old,
my memory is usually very short. I bet Mincy is a good looking young lady. I
probably will be surprised at the change in both her and Marge.

     The other night I got to wondering if I would spend my birthday the same
this year as last. (I hope not.) I then wonder how old I was and wasn't sure
until I counted on my fingers. Including the thumb I covered one hand exactly
five times which makes me feel a bit old. A quarter of a century.
                                        Love from
                                        An Old Man
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] [Ens.] Thos. F. Beck
