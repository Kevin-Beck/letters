                                                  12-10-42
Dear Folks,
     We hit some rough water yesterday and for some unknown reason I didn't
get while most of the crew did. Of course I was conscious of my [crossed out:
stomach] belly but still ate every meal and keep them. Otherwise, everything
as usual. I am learning a little.

     I might get shore leave to-night and if I do I think I will try to get a
haircut. The last one I had was [crossed out] while I was at home. I am
looking quite shaggy now. I did some trimming myself and uncovered my ears.

[--- 421210pg2.jpg ---]

     I have been getting plenty of sleep. We anchored all night and since
everyone was feeling under the weather I laid down about 7:00 and didn't get
up until 7:00 this morning. One nice thing about this ship is that you
[crossed out] all you take off to go to bed is your shoes & cap. It makes
dressing & undressing much faster & simpler. I did manage to get some hot
water from the cook so I could shave to-day. A shower would go nice now but I
am afraid to loose my insulation covering of dirt. All this lack of modern
conveniences doesn't bother me yet.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
