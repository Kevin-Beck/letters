[Printed letterhead: ship's wheel framing a battleship, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

                                                  12/14/42
Dear Mother & Dad,
     The topics we aren't allowed to write about take in about everything but
our health. I feel fine - getting lots to eat & plenty of sleep. I do wish we
were in a warmer climate so I could spend my time sleeping under the sun
rather than in my bunk. Except for one night we have spent all our time at
anchor. There is absolutely nothing for me to do except supervise the men do
nothing. I thought I got away from that life when I left Solomons but all I
have done is change Ports.

     Saturday & Sunday nights I went into Norfolk to see an up to date movie -
"Road to Moroco" & "White Cargo." It is really marvelous how the vitaphone has
progressed. I also learned another thing at Solomons - movie film begins to
wear out after 15 years of service.

[--- 1942-12-14_tom-to-mother-and-dad_p02.jpg ---]

     I heard two conflicting rumors to-day. One, we are going to get a three
days leave at Xmas and second, we are leaving here shortly for a few weeks
training. I don't believe anything anymore until it happens.

     I finally got my hair cut and I don't think I will have to worry for
awhile. I had it cut down short [crossed out: enough] so I [crossed out: won't]
won't have to even bother combing it. All I need [crossed out] now is a bath -
haven't had one since I left Solomons. I also haven't even had my underwear
off - it gets a little chilly at night.

     Would someone please buy Janny a gift as I don't have any means of doing
so here. I will reimburse the party that does.

     I haven't received any mail for a week but I know that isn't your fault. I
will get it eventually.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
