                                                  Jan. 26, 1943
Dear Mother,
     To day has been a busy one for all of us. There are so many routine duties
that I didn't realize existed [crossed out] besides some special work that I
never seem to catch up. So far it has been quite interesting and I think it
will be more so from now on.

     I still think I should have my whites with me. An officer should have a
complete wardrobe so I might as well complete mine. If you haven't allready
shipped them perhaps the suits & shoes should be in seperate boxes for easier
handling. You suit yourself as you probably [crossed out] more about shipping
regulations than I do. Also it is foolish to send any mail by air since it
won't be delivered any quicker.
     I had better close now. Will write again soon
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Address, circled at bottom left:]
     USS LCI(L) 345
     Postmaster N.Y., N.Y.
