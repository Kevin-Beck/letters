                                                  7/8/42
Dear Mother,
     Just disregard that card - I received your letter to-day. I thought the
last mail was in when I sent the card. I thought you had forgotten me, my
roommate has received two letters so far. The Officer of the Deck who brought
your letter in was Glenn Scott. I had a nice talk with him and he appears
like a good fellow. I haven't seen Geo. Wagemhorst [?] yet.

     The math. we are getting is just review for me, but the other courses
are tough. One [crossed out] requires a complete knowledge of all types of
ships and the other one is about Naval Organization. It is all memory work.
The other classes are physical.

     My 4th wasn't much. We

[--- 1942-07-08_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

drilled in the morning, watch a parade in the afternoon, and has a U.S.O.
entertainment in the evening.

     It really isn't as bad as I thought it would be. We have to [crossed out:
march] march to class, etc., but we get a liberty for [crossed out] 1 1/2
hours before evening mess every day. We can't go far but it is nice to get out
of the rooms. The Officers are all swell and they don't yell at us or ride us
like I thought they would. The fellow are O.K. and there are all types, ages,
and of different occupations. A former school teacher was just in and asked me
about some math. I am beginning to realize how fortunate I am getting this
chance and I am going to stick it out if I possibly can. I haven't had my
physical exam yet but I hear it is a snap. If you can walk in you pass.

     These pictures I am enclosing are of some the fellows that room near me.
They are the ones I know best and round around with.

[--- 1942-07-08_tom-to-mother_p03.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

[Printed letterhead: eagle, shield and anchors, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

     The group picture reading from left to right are Spooner, Toledo -
McGuire, Conn. - Cramer, Indiana, - ___ O'Brien, my roommate. The school
teacher from Providence took the pictures. You can figure the other one out.

     You made two mistakes in your letter. You mentioned Columbia and forgot
my room number. Please don't do it again because I will get demerits for it.
We get bounced with 50 demerits. If there are any urgent messages you can call
the official number and ask for the Officer-of-the-Deck and I will get the
message. This for emergency only. Otherwise call one of the pay phone numbers
(I don't know them now, but will send them later) at certain hours - during
mess, (6-8, 12-1, 4-7, 9:30-10). If I am not in leave you number and

[--- 1942-07-08_tom-to-mother_p04.jpg ---]

I can call back. We are allowed visitors but it is best to come at 4 during
the weekday and in the afternoon Saturdays and Sundays. Let me know if you
are coming because I have liberty then.

     I have to start studing how to talk navy talk. It is difficult to get
accustomed to some of the terms, especially telling time on 24 hours base.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom

     Some of the boys are making the same complaint about the coffee [?]
Isabel used to make.
