                                                  Tuesday Eve.
                                                  Aug. 24, 1943
Dear Mom,
     I received mail about five days ago and this is the first chance I have to
answer. I thought I would have a lot of mail to-day but was fooled. It isn't
coming through. I guess its priority isn't high enough.

     I enjoyed the articles by Ernie Pyle especially the way he treats the sea
going barges and the officers. I quite agree with him in a lot of respects. It
is strange you haven't seen pictures of the LCI's as quite a few papers
carried their pictures. We were even mentioned in Time a few months ago.

     The stag party sounded like a big affair. Practically everyone must have
been there. When you mentioned

[--- 1943-08-24_tom-to-mom_p02.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

the refreshments I couldn't help think of what a beautiful hangover I would of
had. Hot dogs always were hard on my stomach. I am sorry about Nunks.

     This was the first I heard about Tucker joining the Navy. Once before you
mentioned he was leaving and I took it for granted that it was the Army. Is he
going in as a "Boot" or will he get a rate. It seems to me that he has enough
experience to go in with some rating. At least I am sure he will be sent to
school and not be just a seaman.

     I hope Glenn Scott likes his new assignment. The big ships have their
advantages and their disadvantage. Of course we of the small craft have our
opinions of the men that come of the large ships. I don't imagine I would
raise a kick if

[--- 1943-08-24_tom-to-mom_p03.jpg (marked "-3-") ---]

I were transfered. I am about ready for a soft birth and an easy life for
awhile.

     I finally found out where Geo. Wagenhorst is and his address. I guess we
have been working to-gether and didn't know it. I am going to write to him and
see if we can't get to-gether some time.

     I am glad Sally received her ring although it sounds like Johnny will be
leaving soon.

     I have about two months pay on the books and one of these days I expect to
collect my travel allowance from Norfolk to Houston. I never got around to
handing my orders in. It will amount to about $100.

     I expect news of the wedding soon so shall save my arm until then.
                                        Love to all
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Beck, T.F.
