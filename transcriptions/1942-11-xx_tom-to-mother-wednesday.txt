                                                  Wednesday
                                                  NOV 1942
Dear Mother,

     I haven't heard a word from home yet but I suppose it will be like the
rest - get a bunch of letter all in one day. I hope that it is the mailmans
fault and that there is nothing wrong at home. It is impossible to get a call
out of here at a decent hour. It either has to be early or late.

     There really isn't much news about this place. We were assigned a
Commanding officer and seven more men and our routine is [crossed out] the
same. The scuttlebutt has it that our ship will be commissioned Dec. 25 but
we will be aboard a couple weeks before. A nice Christmas present. We pick
the ship up at New Orleans or some other Gulf port. My assignment

[--- page 2: 1942-11-xx_tom-to-mother-wednesday_p02.jpg ---]

isn't definite yet so I still might get home for the holidays.

     I guess I should have gotten married. Most of the staff jobs were given
to married men. The pay is higher & they can't live to-gether so it is a good
set up except for one thing. You probably can guess it - except for a couple
good Irishmen, all the soft jobs are going to the Jews. I think life would be
more pleasant fighting the Japs than working with the Jews.

     Chet & I have stuck to-gether so far but he has another type ship and
expects to leave any day. I think I am good for at least two more weeks here.
I think we can get to-gether during maneuvers in the Gulf.

     That is all for now. Will try to get some hot releases for to-morrow.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
