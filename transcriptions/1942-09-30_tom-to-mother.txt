                                                  30 September 1942
Dear Mother,
     I am going on watch to-night and [crossed out] have a few spare minutes
to write. If you get a chance there is an article in Sat. Eve. Post [Oct. 3,
inserted above] about the Navy that we are to read and it might explain what
my job will be. I think the second article will be about amphibious tactics
and I hope to learn something from it. You might find it interesting.

     There is no word on where graduation will be held, but I am sure it will
be on the 21st. The ceremony will last about an hour so I think I will be able
to leave here about noon. One of the fellows here is marrying a girl from
Warren, O. Saturday, 23rd and I am invited. I would like to go very much.

     I haven't done anything about uniforms yet but I think I will

[--- 1942-09-30_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

buy some this week end. I suppose I will need more khaki than any other. At
least I don't think I will have much use for whites.

     About 400 of us have received assignments so far. I am glad I was the
first because the suspense is getting some of them. Every day there is a mad
rush to the bulletin board. A lot of the fellow don't like the idea of
Amphibian school but I think will be a good thing. The only thing I am afraid
off is that there will be so many of us there that we will be penned up like
we are here. I guess a little gold doesn't mean too much at these places.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
