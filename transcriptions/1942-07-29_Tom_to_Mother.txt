                                                  7/29/42
Dear Mother,
     We received our second typhoid shot to-day. I think it must have been a
double header because I could feel it taking a hold within a few minutes. My
arm isn't very stiff yet but I expect it to be quite sore shortly.

     I have missed the first conduct report (demerits) but I expect to be on
the tree. I don't know communications. I can take blinker but not fast enough.

     I imagine you had a good time in Wheeling. I am sorry I couldn't have
been there - I haven't seen that end of the family for some time. The letter
reminded me that the Army has a larger grasp on the family than the Navy does.
About the only thing that proves is that it takes about 10 good Army men to
make one poor Navy man.

[--- 420729Pg2.jpg ---]

     Next Wednesday is graduation for the midshipmen on the other ships. I
suppose it is a big affair and we will have to parade. These boys will be
replace August 11 and also another hall will be opened. I think we will be
offered a chance to transfere to the Prairie State and I think I will decline.
It is an old battleship (the Illinois) stationary in cement with a barn-like
structure on the main deck. It would be more practical but the living
conditions and studying conditions aren't so hot. Maybe they are too hot
because most everything takes place below and the only ventilation are the
small port holes.

     The tailor came back to get my uniform for alterations. I hope to look
more military this week-end.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
