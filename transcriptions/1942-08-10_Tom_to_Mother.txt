                                                  10 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     I received your letter this afternoon and I hope I am not to late in
replying. I am on watch to-day and am quite certain I won't get another one
this week. I won't be able to see you Thursday because I have to have
pictures taken during the short liberty. I can see you for about 1/2 hour
Friday at 5:15. On Saturday we have liberty [?] from 3:15 to 5:45 on Sunday
provided I miss the trees. If I don't I have to be in at 1:00 and can get
back out at 8:00 Sunday morning. That really doesn't matter so much.

     If you want to call when [you] get here John had a list of numbers.

[--- 420810pg2.jpg ---]

Don't call the first number. In case he lost them here are a couple:

     UNI- [crossed out] versity  4-9273
                                 4-9539
                                 4-9635

Isabel can tell you what to do when you get and also about week-day visits.
Please come if possible.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
