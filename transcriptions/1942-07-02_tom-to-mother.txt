[Printed letterhead: eagle, shield and anchors, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

                                                  7-2-42
Dear Mother,

     So much has happened that I am still in a fog. I haven't much time
before taps so I will wait till later to give you the details. I arrived in
time for lunch and spent all afternoon getting my uniform. It is khakie like
the army with officer's cap with a white, blue of khakie top. They are much
better than I expected.

     My room is on the 13th deck (floor) and I am getting in condition
climbing the ladders. My room mate is an Irishman, O'Bryan [?], from Syracuse.
He is fine so far. I have to close now. Will write as soon as possible.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
(over)
