                                                  5 October 1942
Dear Mother,
     I suppose I had better plan on graduating. I am still just a bit afraid
but can't do any thing about it. I bought uniforms Sunday including
everything but shirts & shoes. I got in on a good deal, I hope. One of the
fellows knows a wholesaler in Baltimore and he saved us at least $50. From
what I hear the cloth is standard every where so I think it was allright.

     The invitations were passed out to-day and each fellow get two. If some
one else wants to come I think I might be able to get a couple more. The
exercises are to be held in Riverside Church [crossed out] Wednesday morning
the 21st. The cards are necessary for admission so I will keep them here.
Please let me know as soon as possible if anyone else is coming.

[--- 1942-10-05_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     I have been very neglectful about acknowledging receipt of you gifts -
cookies, popcorn, & candy. I really appreciated them and I think the other
fellows do to because they don't last long. I also received Dad's letter and
the bank statements and am going to do as he suggested.

     Having nothing better to do during the sermon last night I read the
program. They held communion yesterday afternoon and among the new members
was a Paul M. Doty, Jr. I think it might be Doty from Chicora.

     One thing that has been bothering me and that is luggage. They recomend
not to get good stuff but to use a wooden box or paste board box because of
storage facilities at bases. I think I will try to get some boxes from the
stores around here and express most of the stuff. It will be difficult moving
all the junk that is accumulating.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
