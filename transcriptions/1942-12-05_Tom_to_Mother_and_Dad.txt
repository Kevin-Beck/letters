                                                  12-5-42
Dear Mother & Dad,
     We are now in the midst of a very strict muster. All men at the base must
be accounted for. It all started at 0800 this morning. There are five officers
detailed to each barracks to muster the men & to watch them. The only time
anyone could leave the barracks was for chow. It is now 3:30 and there is no
relief in sight. I don't know why we must stand by so long.

     There is nothing new about when I leave here nor about Christmas leave. If

     It is 10:30 now and am sorry about the interruption. An officer came in and
yelled that crew 3081 will leave tomorrow at 0800. I have been busy get the
crew ready & packing my own things. There are still only 2/3 of the required
number of men & only 1/3 of the officers - that is me. I will have my hands
full for awhile as they are now. Will have to cut this short. From now on my
mail is censored (I am the censor for my crew) so there won't be many details.
     My new address is:

[--- 421205pg2.jpg ---]

          U.S.S. - LCI(L) #218
          c/o Postmaster
          New York, N.Y.

This address will only last for about two weeks. Will let you know of any
change.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
