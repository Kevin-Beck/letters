[Printed letterhead: U.S. NAVAL STATION, Grand Trunk Pier, Portland 3, MAINE]

                                                  15 March 1945
Dear Mother,
     Your idea of coming up the first week in April sounds good. It is a lovely
spring to-day and if it holds out you will have no complaints on the weather.
You make the plans and let me know and I will be ready for you. My
entertainment committee won't be here. Lane is being transferred the last of
the month. Also be prepared for sudden cancelation because I have a feeling
some changes will be made and they may be soon but I doubt it. More likely in
a month or two. I will be waiting for whatever you say.

[--- 1945-03-15_tom-to-mother-portland_p03.jpg (marked "II") ---]

[Printed letterhead: U.S. NAVAL STATION, Grand Trunk Pier, Portland 3, MAINE]

     It is too bad about the McClungs moving because they will be missed. The
better position should compensate for the move.

     Received a letter from Geo. and I gather he is being well schooled in one
duty - how to be a socialite. All he talked about was his liberty which I am
glad he is getting plenty of it. More power to him.

     The license plate arrived this morning.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
