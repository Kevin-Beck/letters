                                                  20 February 1944
Dear Mum,
     Again I am a little late in answering your letters but [crossed out]
better late than never. Has Geo. W. been out to see you yet? He said he would
and I know you will feel a lot better after you see him.

     I wish that you were right with your mathematical solution but getting
[a] job [?] depends upon more than just arithmetic. Some of the factors are the
outfit, Commanding Officer, Flotilla Commander, and type of work performed,
etc. I don't rate high in all those.

     We should hit a liberty port soon, first for 12 months. Wine, women, &
song. I expect to have some fun.

     In just another week I will have another anniversary which I guess
doesn't mean a hell of a lot. Just that much more time put in out here.

     The news from the Central Pacific sounds very good. They aren't

[--- 440220pg2.jpg ---]

losing much time up there and I hope the pace continues. I haven't heard much
about the European Front except that it isn't easy going. The fact that they
are going means a lot.

     Just to mention the lighter side of life, I received letters from both
Mari and Sally. I found out a lot of things I didn't know. It was really
amusing. I hope Sally stays in Butler awhile. I really don't think I have much
to do with it, it is just a matter of principle. It would be fun to be home
now.

     I haven't much left to say and very little time. Have to write to Mari &
Sally to-night so shall secure.
                                        My Love to All
                                             Tom
