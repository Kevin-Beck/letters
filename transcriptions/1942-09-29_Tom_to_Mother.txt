                                                  29 September 1942
Dear Mother,
     It was grand talking to you and Dad last night. I wanted to call anyway
and I thought I had a good excuse last night. If I didn't explain very fully
over the 'phone I will do so now, although there isn't much to it. Our
assignments came in this week and they are posting them as fast as possible.
There were about 50 listed yesterday and 150 to-day. A very large majority
are going to Norfolk - some to west coast, Florida, and inland to Diesel
school. No one as yet has been assigned to any big ships, not even
destroyers.

     A few of the fellows are taking it rather hard - they think they are sunk
now. One big fellow on the deck was planning on going to Diesel school (near
his home) and then instructing. He thinks he got a dirty deal. John may
remember seeing a great big boy playing ball. Well that

[--- 420929pg2.jpg ---]

fellow is practically in tears. I don't know why they joined the Navy in they
want to stay on land and not see any action. Now that school is about over
and we have to do a job we signed up for it is strange to see how some react.
I still think they made a mistake here by judging a man by his mental
capacity. They lost a lot of good men.

     We aren't told much about Amphibian school so I don't know whether or not
it is a secret. From what we have been told we will be in charge of landing
barges. I imagine they are starting plans for the 2nd front and that means we
will have until next spring anyway. The school is new and we are getting in
on the ground floor which I think is a good break for advancement. I may even
be in charge of my own ship even tho it is a mud scow. As I said before I
don't know how much of this to be repeated so you had better not tell anyone.

     About 2 minutes until taps. So will close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
