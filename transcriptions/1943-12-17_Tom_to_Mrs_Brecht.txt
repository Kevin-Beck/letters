                                                  U.S.S. LCI(L) 62
                                                  17 December 1943
Dear Mrs. Brecht,
     I received a Christmas package from the Women's Missionary Society and I
wish that you would tell them that I appreciate their kindness and
thoughtfulness. The included articles are practical and I will have much use
for them.

     Thank you very much for your generosity and for thinking of me at this
time.

     I hope that you and Reverend Brecht have a happy and prosperous New Year.
                                        Sincerely,
                                             Tom Beck

[Written at bottom left:] Ens. Thos. F. Beck
