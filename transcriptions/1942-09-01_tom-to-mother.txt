                                                  1 September 1942
Dear Mother,
     I am very sorry I forgot about your birthday. Every day is the same here
and I don't keep up on what goes on outside. I will try to remember the next
one.

     I was on watch yesterday and we had a lot of extra work to do. Our
schedule was changed and to-day was a full and right now I am play out. I was
always used to taking it easy right after exams but they can't see it here.
Not only are they going after us harder but we have to make up all work we
missed during finals. I feel as if I am heading up a blind alley. Tomorrow is
another bilge day and if they crack down as as before I doubt if there will
be many left.

     I think I pick the wrong part of the Navy. Any half wit who doesn't know
any thing about any thing can come here and instruct us. If it wasn't for a
few of

[--- 1942-09-01_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg (parent folder) ---]

the officers I would think I was at Polk.

     We get a cruise on Thursday. We are then out in the harbor for about 8
hours and get some practical work. At least we will see water and feel a sea
breeze.

     I received a letter from Betty and she said she and John are planning
coming to New York. I am trying to find out for sure how I stand on Sunday
watches. It is tough to get information like that out of the officers. I hope
that we can plan a week end.

     I have mess in a few minutes so had better close.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
