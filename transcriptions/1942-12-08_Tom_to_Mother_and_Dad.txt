                                                  12/8/42
Dear Mother & Dad,
     I left on such short notice that I didn't have time to stock up on
writing paper and this is all I have. I will be getting some other soon, I
hope.

     There is very little to tell you even if I was allowed to. Most of the
time is spent getting the crew organized so that they can learn something. I
also have to make out a watch bill and pick working parties. I might at that
so far I am getting plenty of sleep, eating heartily, and am feeling fine -
no seasickness yet.

     I received a box of cookies from Florella [?] and we have been enjoying
them quite regularly.

[--- page 2: 421208pg2.jpg ---]

Aunt Hazel sent me some candy which was quickly consumed. I am certainly
getting my share of gifts and I appreciate every one of them.

     I have one more letter to write for the Red Cross clothing Mrs. Mortimer
gave me. I had to wait until cold weather and then space the letters so they
will think I received them here at different times. I don't like to deceived
them like that but I think there morale will be higher if they think that I
received the stuff here. I am glad to have them no matter where I got them.
     Will write soon.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
