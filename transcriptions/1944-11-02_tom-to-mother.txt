                                                  2 Nov. 1944
Dear Mother,
     I have the duty to-night and am ready to secure so this will be very
brief. I received yours & Isabel's letters and must admit I do feel relieved.
Glad to hear every one is fine.

     The main reason for this hurried note is that I moved last night. I now
live at 175 State St. telephone 3-0804. I felt a change wouldn't hurt so I
went in with another officer. Living alone gets on my nerves.

     Also, received a package from W.C.M.S. marked Christmas package. Ate the
contents. Should I thank them now or at Xmas.

     Will write a letter later.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written vertically in left margin, with arrow to the address:]
(I am still in the same status in regards to [crossed out] orders)
