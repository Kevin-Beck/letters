                                                  23 May
Dear Mom,
     I have been moved from my old quarters, temporarily I hope. The SOQ
became crowded so another Ensign and myself were moved to House 8. We still go
to SOQ for sick call and meals.

     House 8, by the way, is the maternity ward and for the sick nurses and
Waves. The room is nice but at times there is a lot of noise. The building is
barred shut at night to keep the boys out. I had to laugh when the nurse told
me that. I probably will be back to the old quarters in a few days.

     Went to Boston Sunday to see a ball game. It wasn't much of a game but was
nice to get away

[--- 450523pg2.jpg ---]

from this place.

     The sun was out yesterday and I spent a couple of hours taking advantage
of it. I am a light pink, tinged with yellow, but didn't get burned enough to
bother me much. I intend to get some more to-day if the sun comes out.

     Received a letter from Mr. West inviting me to look him up if I go to
Boston. If I get down that way again I would like to meet him.

     I gather my moving means the Malaria attack is over and that it is just a
matter of waiting for the survey. The Doc hasn't said a word about it.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
