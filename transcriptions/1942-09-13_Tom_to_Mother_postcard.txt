Return address (handwritten):  U.S.N.R. Midshipmen's School
                               New York, N.Y.
Printed: The BILTMORE, Madison at Forty-Third, New York, N.Y.
Postage: "Free" written in the stamp box
Addressed to:  Mrs. John A. Beck
               Karns City, Pa.

Dear Mother,
     I am on my way to Hackensack for dinner. Pat Hill has invited me out.
     The game yesterday was wonderful.
                                             Tom
