                                                  7/22/42
Dear Mother,
     Bit by bit I am getting I am gathering information about the first
bilge. The [crossed out] Navy says about 90 left to-day and that 100 more
will go before graduation. If I was supestitious I would be scared. Our deck,
13, lost 13 boys, but both boys in 1313 stayed.

     To-morrow we are to be sworn in as midshipmen and also start taking
shots. Our courses are underway now and my toughest is communication. I know
most of the flags but can't get the Morse Code.

     We have the week-end off and I don't know what to do. There are plenty
of invitations to parties, dances, clubs, and free tickets to most every-
thing. The one invitation that [crossed out] takes

[--- 420722pg2.jpg ---]

my fancy is a beer party Saturday afternoon. It has been so long since I
have tasted any that I may not like it, but I don't think that will happen.
That's all for now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
