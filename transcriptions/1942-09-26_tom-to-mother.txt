                                                  26 September 1942
Dear Mother,
     I am still not certain what to do about uniforms. One of the officers
gave a lecture to-day and told us to buy the minimum until we are sure where
we are going. He said that in some assignments we won't even need khaki, we
will wear overalls. He should know so I think I will follow his advice. He
also mentioned graduation. There are three places where it can be held and
only one, Riverside Church, is large enough to accomodate any guests. I will
let you know as soon a I hear anything definite and if it is impossible for
you to see the ceremony you may not want to come. Also, we will receive our
orders any time from next week until the day before graduation. Judging from
the last

[--- 1942-09-26_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

class we will have to report on the following Monday. That will give me a
few days at home unless I am sent to the West Coast.

     It doesn't seems the same here. The work has eased up and so have the
men. There is one instructor I can't stand and I couldn't or wouldn't work
for him. He put me on the tree with a 2.4 (2.5 is passing). It is my first
tree and I am sorry I made it - I was planning on getting through with out
any. If we change instructors next week I am sure I can get off. It really
doesn't mean much because the overall average is they one they want.

     Navigation is quite interesting now. We are starting a theoretical 6-day
trip across the Atlantic and will used practically every thing they have
taught us. It will be a good review. In Ordinance they are teaching us more
about small boats and are even letting us touch real guns.

[--- 1942-09-26_tom-to-mother_p03.jpg ---]

[Printed letterhead: eagle, shield and anchors, "UNITED STATES NAVY"]

Some of the fellows have never handled the rifle or pistol. We will get some
work with machine guns and small 3 inch guns.

     I just got back from class and the liberty bell just sounded.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
