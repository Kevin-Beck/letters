                                                  3 April 44
Dear Mum,
     I wrote to John to-night but I can't neglect you. I got the habit of every
time I write letters I always include one to you.

     Marjorie's birthday present came the other day. She knows more about the
sea than I thought she did. The good old invigorating salt spray is taking it
toll on my slippers. I will be needing a pair soon.

     I can't say that I have been doing much - getting by with food and sleep.
I must say the Navy feeds well if you don't mind dehydrated foods. I am
getting used to them.

     I am feeling fine and I think I have gained a pound or two.
