                                                  24 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     We received our exam schedule and it isn't so bad. We have four exams,
two on Thursday and two on Friday. Each exam is two hours long and there are
a lot of questions they can ask in two hours.

     O'Brien and I spent Saturday at Coney Island. It was a lot of fun and I
think I might try it again some week end. I am going to try to get to the
football game between the Green Bay Packers & the Dodgers next Saturday night.
To-day is ideal football weather - the wind is from the [crossed out: East]
West and I suppose you had this cool spell first.

     The liberty bell just rang and I haven't much [crossed out] more to say
so had better close. I will have to spend most of my time studying so may not
write again for a few days.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
