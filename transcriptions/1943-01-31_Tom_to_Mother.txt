                                                  Jan. 31, 1943
Dear Mother,

     Complications have set in. The executive officer on another ship is sick
so I am to be transfered. I don't like the idea at all but I guess that is
something I will have to get used to. I hate to leave all the friends I have
on here. One redeamable factor is that the ship is a few months older and no
doubt has experienced men. It is not too definite yet but I think the
transfer will go through. You can draw your own conclusions when they can't
wait a few days for a man to get out of Sick Bay.

     I received a letter from Margorie to-day. I am sorry I can't get to see
her play basket ball. From what everyone has written and from the write-up
she must be very good. It is too

[--- 430131pg2.jpg ---]

[bad] I won't be in Houston to see the Roudybush [?] wedding. I would like to
go back to Houston anyway.

     Bertha Beck send me a box of candy the other day. It was hard candy and it
tasted very good. Everyone has been good to by sending stuff and by writing to
me.

     I was paid yesterday by check and I don't know what to do with it. It came
to $366 and I don't have any use for all that but I do need some of it. If I
can get to town to cash it I think I will send a money order home.

     I will let you know as soon as possible if I make a change. The other ship
is #62.

     I will also write as often as possible although it is difficult to fill a
letter even now.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

USS LC(I)(L) #345
c/o Postmaster
N.Y., N.Y.
