                                                  Thurs.
                                                  Aug 19/43
Dear Mother,
     I was ready to hit the sack and was putting something away when I came
across some pictures. My last letter just went out yesterday and I didn't
intend to write for a few days but I changed my mind. My assortment of pictures
is small but very valuable. They include the ones of Libby's wedding, 4 of the
kid, 2 of you, 6 of the kids & the magnolia, 1 of Luke & Lena & 1 of just Luke,
and last but not least one of Mari.

     The pictures bring back a lot of memories and they seem to take me much
closer to home. When I look at them I wonder just what each one of you are
doing and how you are getting along. There are times when they make

[--- 1943-08-19_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

kind of homesick but usually they cheer me up as I feel closer to you. I never
before cared much for many pictures but now I am not hesitating to ask you to
send a lot of them to me. I would like to have a better one of you. See what
you can do about it.

     I am surprised at Isabel. An Old Sea B (what is the proper word for a
female Sea Dog) like her should know that single bars come in both gold and
silver. Also, that the Navy is full of "Mustangs". While on the subject how is
the Boot getting along. He wrote once, shortly after arriving in Lewisburg,
and I am expecting another letter from him. He is almost as bad as the rest of
your

[--- 1943-08-19_tom-to-mother_p03.jpg ---]

family. I shouldn't have said that because I realize that one letter from the
house is enough and you are really doing your part. I don't mean that I don't
like to hear from them.

     In that clipping your sent about the parties, etc. for John & Fran there
was one other name that was prominent. It seems like one Mrs. J. H. McClung
Jr. grabbed all the prizes. Did you have any trouble with her around the
wedding gifts? Jackie must being wrecking the house.

     I had better knock off and get some shut-eye. I am still thinking of you
and hope that you are as fit as I am.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom

[Written at bottom left:] Beck, T.F. ENS.
