                                                  8 August 1942
Dear Mother,
     Another week has gone by and I missed the tree. We also had Captain's
Inspection this morning and I passed that. When he inspects you he really
gives you the once over. I know now how a girl feels when she passes a gang
of Navy men.

     I think I will go down town for a while to-night and come back here to
sleep. I have a ticket for a ball game a Yankee Stadium to-morrow. Last week
I saw the Giants and the Cards [crossed out] play. I didn't recognize the
place. I guess the last time I was there I was in to big a hurry to look
around.

     There is to be another 5% bilge Monday. I am not worried academicly

[--- 1942-08-08_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

and I don't know [of] any other things they could get me on. If you don't
hear from you will know I am still here.

     It has been rather [crossed out] quiet around here since graduation but
I imagine it will be like a mad-house when the new gang gets here next week.
There will be about a 1000 of them and I suppose we will have to help during
the indoctrination period.

     I have a class in a few minutes - my last before liberty [?] - so had
better close. Try to come up soon before it gets too tough here.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
