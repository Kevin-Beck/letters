                                                  Feb. 20, 1943
Dear Mother,
     Here is another change of address. I think the last one I gave you will
work o.k. but use the new one from now on. They say any other one will be
returned to sender. You might also add the Amphibious Force although I don't
think it is necessary.

     One week from to-day will be just another day, I am afraid. I don't feel
much older but I hope I act a little older. I guess I am getting up in years.
Perhaps I should ask for a transfer as this is supposed to be a young man's
war. My only wish now is that I don't have to spend two birthdays in the
service.

[--- 1943-02-20_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

     There are [crossed out] a lot of questions I want to ask and you probably
have answered them all by now. Is Isabel still at home or is she in Butler, or
did she go through with the Waves or Waacs deal? How is Janny getting along
without Bill and is she still in school. Does John Mc. like his new job and is
John B. still single (he should have one earned by now)? I imagine George is
still working hard & Marg is still playing basketball. How about yourself -
still collecting old silk and showing them how to run the rationing board. Do
the people still argue or have they given up?
     That should keep you busy for awhile.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
