                                                  Tuesday
                                                  Morning
Dear Mother,
     We got in about 5:00 Sunday, moved in, and then took Billy down to the
station. He took the 6:00 train, so he couldn't eat dinner with us. We ate at
the 7th Ave. Phyllis was here when I got there, but she left me the room I
wanted. I met my 'little sister' right away, and I like her lots. She's very
backward, but she's cute & pretty.

     The girl next door met Bill at Shadyside Sunday, and said she thot he
looked homesick. So she's going to help me call him today. Peggy Bishop says
that

[--- undated_janny-to-mother-pcw_p02.jpg ---]

Keith & Jean are having him out for dinner, I think this Sunday.

     The check didn't come in the morning mail, but I told them that I wanted
to register anyway. I have almost decided to take Chemistry instead of
Psychology. It will be much harder (more work), but I almost need it for Home
Ec. If you think it's wrong let me know, and I can change. Maybe John would
know. I have no Sat. classes, but 4 on Mon. Wed. & Fri.

     I like my room real well, and I'm having lots of fun fixing it up. I would
like to have a chair and floor lamp if you think you could spare one from
home. I have green & deep rose

[--- page(s) apparently missing ---]

[--- undated_janny-to-mother-pcw_p03.jpg ---]

in Daddy's car. I left a large jar of Freshen [?] in my dresser drawer. I
suppose I'll need my Bible for Religion. It's in Isabel's room. I put the
pin-hooks from my drapes in a little box, but I don't know where it is. It was
in the box that the drapes were in which was in the basement, but they're not
there. Please don't forget those shoes that are in the box in my room. The
other things are my tennis raquet and a can-bottle opener.

     I'll be home the week-end after next, & I may bring Phyllis home.
     Everything's going fine. I'm going to have plenty of money. We're going
shopping tomorrow afternoon.
     I'll write again soon.
                                        Love
                                             Janny
