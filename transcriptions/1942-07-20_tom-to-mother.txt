                                                  7/20/42
Dear Mother,
     It won't be long now till we hear the news, good or bad. The mate of our
deck, a midshipman, was inspecting rooms to-day if we know how to keep our
room ship shape. He said that the midshipmen are leaving our "ship" and we
are to be on our own from to-morrow on. I gather from that we will be sworn
in as midshipmen in a day or two. I also heard the boys get their walking
papers Tuesday night. Since very few demerits are given out I think they will
start to crack down on us. We started our main course to-day.

     As far as my marks are concerned for the first course I am not sure of
them. We are not allowed to ask the profs. for them. We corrected two exams
in class and I heard indirectly from the fellows who corrected mine

[--- 1942-07-20_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

that I got a 3.2 & a 2.9. Perfect is 4. The instructors graded the math and
I think I hit that exam. We are also graded on aptitude, which takes in every
thing. I can't see how [crossed out] they can judge individually 900 men in
two weeks. It is claimed that they do it. I think I told you about the lower
50% on our entrance exam being interviewed. I miss that so far. I also heard
that they kick a certain percentage out every 3 weeks or 4 times in all.

     I just got the dope on liberties. If nothing is wrong we get Saturday
night till Sunday evening off (within a radius of 50 miles), if we are "on
the tree", below 2.5 in any course we just get Saturday night off, and if we
get demerits we must march them off at the rate of 2 an hour. We do that
Saturday afternoon and if all are worked off in the three hours we are free.
It sounds complicated and I guess the only way to figure it out is to be a
good boy.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
