                                                  7/14/42
Dear Mother,
     I may repeat a few things too. It is tough remembering what I have
written and to whom I have written. The big problems now is laundry. It is
four days late and all mixed up. I guess the whole mess is the fault of the
Navy - they issued poor ink and it washed out. I didn't do so badly, all I'm
missing is an under-shirt. Some of the fellows asked an officer what they
should do when the laundry doesn't come back. He gave the correct answer,
just stink & wait. I think I might smell sweeter if you would send those
T shirts of mine. The ones you just bought me and a couple I had before. Just
the plain white ones.

     This week ends the indoctrination

[--- 1942-07-14_tom-to-mother_p02.jpg ---]

period. We have final Friday & Saturday. If our grades are high we become
midshipmen, if average they checked for other officer qualities, and if low
we are bilged. I will have to spend most of my spare time studying and I
won't be able to write much till next week if I am still here. The math
doesn't bother me as much as the other courses - they are all memory work.

     We had about two hours of rowing this afternoon. My hands are tougher
than the first time - I don't think I will get any blisters. After rowing we
had signaling with flags. Right now my arms are dead weight.

     I am just getting over a cold. I think I got it from being in a draft
when overheated.

     I had a nice visit with John even tho all we did was walk and sit. I am
used to it but I don't know how John liked it.

                                        Love,
                                             Tom
