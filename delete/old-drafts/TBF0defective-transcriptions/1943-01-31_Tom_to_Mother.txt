                                                  Jan. 31, 1943
Dear Mother,

     Complications have set in. The executive officer on another ship is sick
so I am to be transfered. I don't like the idea at all but I guess that is
something I will have to get used to. I hate to leave all the friends I have
on here. One redeamable factor is that the ship is a few months older and no
doubt has experienced men. It is not too definite yet but I think the
transfer will go through. You can draw your own conclusions when they can't
wait a few days for a man to get out of Sick Bay.
