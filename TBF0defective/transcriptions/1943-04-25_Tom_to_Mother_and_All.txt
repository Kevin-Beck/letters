                                                  April 25, 1943
Dear Mother & All,

     I could start off complaining about the mail but I know it isn't your
fault so I won't go into any details.

     Today started out as all other days and ended the same. There was one
exception and that was dinner - we had roast turkey, stuffing (made from Ritz
crackers), mashed potatoes, peas, gravy, and pumpkin pie for dinner. I was
curious about the feast and was informed it was Easter Sunday. I should have
know because a couple of mornings ago the Eng. officer gave me his bacon. It
is strange how the days come and go so fast that I have no idea what day is
what. I use to be able to tell Sunday as the day of rest from the night before
but not now. It is just another day of long, hard labor. It is also strange
that the more work I have to do the more contented I am (that doesn't include
deck watches). I suppose the answer is that

[--- 430425pg2.jpg (marked "-2-") ---]

the more I have to think about the ship the less time I have to think about
other things, especially the past.

     I do try to forget the past and make plans for the future and that takes
about all my spare time. As I see the future at home again has very few dark
clouds and we all should have few worrys. Of course, that is the future and we
have to let it decide for itself.

     I often thought I was fortunate in being able to see foreign lands and
still get paid for it. I am not too sure now. I will say this that most of it
is beautiful, especially the first [crossed out] view but it loses its beauty
rapidly. I don't think I will ever be foolish enough to spend, or waste, any
money on an ocean voyage. I will whole heartedly back the man that orginated
the phrase "See America First."

     We have heard a few news broadcasts

[--- 430425pg3.jpg (marked "-3-") ---]

from the states and right now it sounds o.k. I hope it continues. It is so
seldom that we can [crossed out] use the radio for such purpose that we are
quite fortunate to get any recent news a couple times a week. It is some
comfort to hear American broadcasts and the crew really goes for the musical
part. I always did find it difficult to keep up with new bands and the recent
hits and [crossed out: now] I am 'way behind. Some night I am going to tune in
on an old fashion hoedown and sit back and enjoy myself.

     I was cleaning out a compartment to-day the one we keep our baggage in. I
went through my bags again and put all the stuff in one bag. You can guess
what most of it was long underwear, heavy sweaters, gloves, scarfs, and woolen
sox. As I was packing them I thought of the two weeks I was aboard ship at
