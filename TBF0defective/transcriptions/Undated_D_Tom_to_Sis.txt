                                                  Sunday,
Dear Sis,

     With my limited vocabulary it is difficult to express just how much I
enjoyed being with you Saturday. I will say it was swell and I think you will
understand what I mean. I do regret that I was in a bad humor. The impression
I left of the base is not very good and I think that under other circumstances
I would have said some nice things. It really isn't so bad if you overlook the
items that I bitched about. Anyway, I think I will leave soon.

     There is nothing new.
Tell the kid I said hello.
                                        Love,
                                             Tom
(over)
